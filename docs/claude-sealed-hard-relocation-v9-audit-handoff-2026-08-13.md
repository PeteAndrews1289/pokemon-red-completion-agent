## What changed

1. Ordinary directional-warp arrival is now derived from the destination trigger. A south-facing
   source adds the inward doorway step only when the paired destination warp is cartridge-
   classified as automatic. Returns retain their measured southward settlement.
2. Boundary ordinary warps require their geometric outward input on every edge. The existing
   cartridge automatic-warp table may then clear that action. This distinguishes map 47's automatic
   top warp from its directional bottom warp.
3. A connection candidate is no longer accepted from its source endpoint alone. When the target
   local graph is available, its arrival must exist and expose an inward edge permitted by the
   preserved traversal mode and current capabilities. This prevents Route 21 water from being
   paired with Cinnabar collision or dry-land coordinates.
4. Schema v9 records the eighth pre-access amendment and supersedes exact v8 digest
   `fe208ac5cf628bcd7301ae500622ae59e39bea271f60d817e2f70f3001fcc5d9`. The model, twelve cases,
   order, endpoints, attempt policy and scoring policy do not change.
