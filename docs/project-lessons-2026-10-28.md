## Seven lessons

1. **Locate the responsible layer before changing weights.** Nine of14 failed
   capture operations ended in knockout signatures without spending a ball.
   The frozen deterministic helper selected Rattata/Tackle by species before
   level. Three operations escaped without ball use; two consumed3balls without
   retention. These are operations, not14throws or14strategic choices. The exact
   throw outcomes remain uncertain. Test the existing nonlethal capture support
   before proposing a learner correction. Goal selection and capture execution
   have different owners.
2. **A legal action missing from the menu cannot be chosen.** Affordable ball
   stock existed, but distant suppliers were unreachable and the local shop was
   omitted. The first Vermilion repair worked natively; its locality restriction
   repeated the failure in Cerulean. Pending v10 offers both verified suppliers
   through existing feasible-route and purchase guards. It passes165 focused
   checks but remains unrun. Data-driven capability coverage is preferable to
   another town-specific strategic exception.
3. **Numerical retention is not native competence.** The17-row correction passed
   its numeric gates, then chose direct Misty and lost twice. Pooled error could
   also improve while a legacy subgroup regressed. Report executor groups,
   actual child-proposed inputs, selected actions and complete payoff separately.
   Do not adjust a consumed gate after observing a failure.
4. **The deployed system includes flags, helpers and timing.** Identical weights
   can receive different features, candidate sets or continuation behavior.
   Public/private entry divergence stopped the broader viability assessment
   before emulation. The shared entry repair now starts this composition, while
   mid/late gameplay remains unassessed. One model SHA is insufficient to define
   a controlled comparison.
5. **Useful preparation has an end-to-end cost.** Winning and gaining XP do not
   show that the intended trainee benefited efficiently. Retain trainee XP share,
   switching, faints, travel, PP, nursing and cash costs. A stronger wild venue
   can still lose time through recovery or give the trainee no XP. Earlier
   actor-aligned comparisons demonstrate local improvement; broader transfer
   remains open.
6. **A no-fit decision can be progress.** Matched Rat-dose evidence already agreed
   with the fixed head, and a prompt grid failed its declared information gate.
   Preserve those negative results. Fit only an informative TRAIN contrast for a
   demonstrated model-owned error. Exposed development losses are not new TRAIN
   labels; sibling snapshots do not become independent origins.
7. **Measure useful play and interventions, not activity volume.** The last block
   spent only6m15s in the native-player clock. Startup verification and copied
   harness work recur; earlier narrow-package work already reduced one measured
   bind29.243→1.131seconds. Reuse qualified mechanisms without bypassing checks.
   Test counts, parameter counts, versions and supported wins do not establish
   sustained autonomous play. There is no controlled productivity comparison
   between coding-model tiers in these changing sessions. Session-count finish
   estimates are unsupported by the current measured throughput.
