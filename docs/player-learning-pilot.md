# Transfer-oriented player learning pilot

> Documentation role: historical scoped plan. The original trial, budget and
> results below retain their date-specific scope. For current work follow
> [HANDOFF](../HANDOFF.md) and latest user direction; this page is not a dispatch.


Pete approved this direction on September25 after the source-supply audit, explicitly
prioritizing eventual Crystal learning. This authorizes preparing the new bounded
assisted curriculum; it does not authorize a whole campaign or opening Crystal.

## Mission check

- Capability: choose progression, preparation, recovery and resource actions from
  their consequences, then replan after a setback.
- Learned authority: the learner selects among actual executable alternatives;
  teachers may prepare and demonstrate TRAIN situations, never silently replace
  an evaluated choice. Forced actions and emergency stops remain labeled.
- Transfer test: withheld source groups and situation combinations in Red first;
  later measure Crystal before adaptation and again after a declared teaching budget.
- Cheapest falsifier: compare the actual inference vectors and pairwise contrasts
  before generating lessons. Identical inputs cannot teach different responses;
  changed inputs alone do not prove useful policy learning.
- Time box: one90-minute implementation slice before reassessment; stop expanding
  the pool if the first small fit cannot show useful train-side retention.
- Stop condition: unresolved source overlap, unavailable executable alternatives,
  unsupported outcome labels or a structural representation failure. Retain failures;
  repair the common interface, not a checkpoint-specific walkthrough.

## Bounded sequence

1. **Representation preflight, no gameplay.** Use the existing inference projection,
   not a second teacher-only vector. Contrast ready/unready, healthy/depleted,
   affordable/unaffordable and scarce/replenishable situations. Keep action costs
   and risks grounded in adapter evidence; do not tune them to force a choice.
2. **Source preparation.** Select only authenticated, authorized source groups.
   Record ancestry, original partition, setup edits and source/state/code identities.
   Assign TRAIN and comparison groups before producing outcomes. Never split siblings
   across partitions. Unknown ancestry is diagnostic-only, not independent evaluation.
   Use short isolated setup with disclosed interventions; no clean-power route factory.
3. **Small TRAIN pilot.** Initial ceiling24single-goal lessons: at most6each for
   story versus development, recovery versus continuation, earning versus spending,
   and acquisition versus resource preservation. These are coverage intentions,
   not a promise that all executors/importers are ready. Validate each family first.
   Stop after the first8attempts if fewer than4yield admissible measured outcomes;
   investigate infrastructure rather than spend the remaining budget blindly.
4. **One candidate fit and train-side retention check.** Preserve the old corpus
   and frozen baseline. Failed played arms retain costs; unplayed arms get no labels.
   Teacher-selected lessons are not comparative policy choices. No tuning on held-out
   results. A failed fit closes this pilot's fit, not all future training.
5. **Bounded comparison.** Before any evaluation access, freeze exact sources,
   baseline/candidate identities, scoring, case order and caps in a private manifest.
   Initial ceiling8paired cases spanning all four decision families; report per-case
   and source-group results, interventions, time/actions, invalid choices and cost.
   Require at least2verified disjoint comparison source groups, neither used to fit
   the candidate or baseline. This is a small development screen, not a statistical
   generalization claim. An unresolved source supply blocks this comparison, not
   ROM-free implementation or explicitly labeled TRAIN-side diagnostics.
6. **Connected play promptly.** If the frozen comparison supports proceeding,
   use at most2new disjoint comparison episodes of at most6goal decisions each.
   Include a prospectively declared recoverable adverse condition; retain natural
   failures without manufacturing a favorable replacement. Measure useful progress,
   resource balance and recovery without operator target selection. Do not make
   exhaustive mastery of every isolated situation a prerequisite to this test.

Each native case must also declare action/frame/wall-time caps no greater than
existing executor bounds before dispatch. These ceilings are not execution manifests:
no source is claimed and no case is ready merely because this document exists.
Publish the smaller first tranche only after source and runtime qualification.
Comparison acceptance must be frozen before opening comparison payloads: illegal
or invented progress is disqualifying; require useful multi-family progress with
no undisclosed assistance, and report actual baseline/candidate differences rather
than demanding100%wins or claiming significance from8cases.

## Explicit experimental-scope decision

The legacy broad curriculum's8TRAIN/4development lineage gate and all of its old
records remain unchanged. It is not a universal prerequisite to engineering or
TRAIN-side learning. This new narrow pilot uses a separately declared small-screen
scope, never claims to pass that legacy gate, and cannot promote whole-player or
cross-title authority by itself. All partition and consumed-trial protections remain.
New filenames, RNG seeds or setup edits never make an old source independent.
Use any known overlapping source only within its authorized partition and declared
diagnostic scope. Do not relabel prior developmental experience as untouched testing.

## What should carry into Crystal

Share goal meanings, readiness/resource features, outcome units, decision memory,
the learner and the training/evaluation loop. Keep map coordinates, RAM addresses,
species/objective IDs and controller sequences inside title adapters/executors.
Creature/move properties may matter; raw IDs must not stand in for their meaning.
Do not claim the current Red policy already transfers. Crystal will need its own
observation/action adapters and changed-mechanic support, followed by zero-shot
measurement and separately reported adaptation. No Crystal access in this pilot.

Track model-versus-teacher ownership at every decision; singleton and safety actions
must not inflate autonomy. Log option set, actual choice/probability when sampled,
policy/feature versions, elapsed time, costs, terminal verification, interventions,
failure reason and next decision. Keep private identities outside policy features.
Current missing mechanics/features remain part of the pre-campaign goal: native
Jynx composite qualification, Game Corner, finite-stock opportunity cost, richer
readiness, economic lesson admission and the live lesson recorder.

## First implementation

September26: the first-eight gate failed at3admissible/8attempts. Pete explicitly
approved [four additional attempts after preflight](player-pilot-continuation-2026-09-26.md).
All original records and the failed gate remain; neither denominator nor original
overall/per-family caps reset. This bounded amendment is not unlimited collection.

`player_decision_probe.py` compares exact existing inference inputs without a model
query, label, emulator or source access. Its synthetic tests expose a useful limit:
raising generic team pressure can change both story/development vectors equally,
leaving their linear contrast unchanged when estimated risks match. This calls for
target-aware preparation features, not extra copies of the same lesson. The probe
is a diagnostic, not a replacement runtime, learner or proof of generalization.
