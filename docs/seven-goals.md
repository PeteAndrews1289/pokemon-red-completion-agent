# Seven work areas

November 19, 2026. Four same-seed development saves reached Champion, the Hall of Fame and all 124 required registrations. All four used external continuations.

| Area | Current question |
|---|---|
| Story and progress | Can one unchanged player reach Champion and the Hall of Fame without external intervention? |
| Collection and registration | Can that same save earn 124, including late evolutions, trades and resource-dependent catches? |
| Battle and preparation | Does the chosen trainee get useful XP and later contribute to the objective? |
| Game economy and travel | Do money, fees, balls and healing opportunities carry through to a successful registration? |
| Runtime and evidence | Can control return after a real loss, with the save, cash and original failure intact? |
| Goal quality and learning | Can the player finish useful work with fewer repeated acquisition and restoration choices? |
| Integrated delivery and transfer | Can clean Red performance repeat on held-out starts and later carry to unfamiliar games? |

Across the four, 63.3% of active time came after the Hall of Fame. The solo attempt had one external intervention at the interview snapshot.
