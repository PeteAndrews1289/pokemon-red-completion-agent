1. Capability: choose a useful reserve or keep a useful active member under
   immunity, low HP, status and multi-opponent attrition. Reuse across trainers
   and later games; no Agatha, species, room or party-slot rule.
2. Learned authority: train only the existing switch and control heads. Preserve
   the admitted damage reference, status/move head, feature schemas, legal-action
   masks. Only qualified opt-in forced mechanics extend continuation. Setup teachers
   may construct TRAIN states, not labels.
3. Transfer: use fresh constructed TRAIN compositions not used for fitting; keep
   actor/opponent species-pair groups disjoint between fit and comparison. Timing
   variants and sibling states remain correlated. A later DEVELOPMENT attempt is
   separate and cannot be used to revise the candidate.
4. Cheapest falsifier: authenticate sources and inventories without gameplay;
   then a small, prospectively selected continuation pilot may show no useful
   alternative return or unsupported prompt construction. Stop before fitting.
5. Time box: one implementation/measurement packet, initially 90 minutes; at most
   one declared fit run, no seed/optimizer sweep, no automatic successor on failure.
   Freeze exact runtime/action/episode budgets before dispatch, not after results.
6. Stop: malformed or reused identity, incomplete branch, unsupported semantics,
   missing retention inventory, model drift, budget exhaustion, or failed gate.
   Retain all results. Do not patch action selection to make a gate pass.
