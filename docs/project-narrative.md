# From a Pokémon teacher to a local learned player

The project began with a question about ownership. A program can finish an RPG if a developer supplies the route. Could a local player choose useful work, see its consequences and carry that judgment forward? Pete made the first target concrete. Start Pokémon Red from power-on, become Champion and register the 124 species available under the project's Red route rules. Leave a record that another reader can verify. [Mission](../MISSION.md) · [Completion contract](completion-contract.md)

## When watching the game changed the question

Pete saw the player pay to enter the Safari Zone, leave with work unfinished and later return. He also saw high-level trainees facing low-level opponents and switches that sent XP to another party member. Those observations changed the measurements. For goal selection we track completion and repeated choices. For training we track the intended Pokémon's XP, travel, battles and healing. For a campaign we keep timestamps and frames at 40 milestones. [Seven work areas](seven-goals.md)

## Four finishes told four different stories

All four same-seed campaigns reached Champion, the Hall of Fame and 124 on their own saves. All four needed repairs or host continuations. Three reached the Hall of Fame within 25 seconds of each other in active wall time. The fourth needed 5h 06m and suffered 19 Silph rival losses before winning. Later, Run 3 took 165 minutes for registrations 75–100. Run 2 took 75 minutes. The four spent 63.3% of their combined active time after the Hall of Fame. [Four-run results and all splits](work-sessions/four-run-splits-2026-11-19.md)

## What one blackout taught us

The team brought the four repair histories together and checked 22 native cases before a new solo start. Squirtle then fainted from field poison on the way to heal. The saved checkpoint was identical to the first stop in the earlier four campaigns. The old code could handle the loss after a restart. The missing piece was the handoff inside the running emulator. Starting the test before the faint exposed the difference. Red charged ₽633, healed the party and returned strategic control to the model. The solo save retained one operator intervention. [Solo recovery](work-sessions/solo-field-blackout-2026-11-19.md)

## What comes next

The solo reached the Hall of Fame at 83:23:08 on the cartridge. Pete set a six-hour story target and parked the save. The first speed-trained battle weights saved 1m 26s across four held-out practice wins and tied the original weights on Erika and Blaine. An earlier whole-badge comparison suggests a larger opportunity. Fighting Blaine immediately took 5m 21s, while preparing first took 1h 11m 23s. Both earned the badge. Farfetch’d gained 4,577 XP and made zero attacks against Blaine. Completing one uninterrupted run and transferring beyond Red remain project goals.
