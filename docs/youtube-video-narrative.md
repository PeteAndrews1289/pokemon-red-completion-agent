# Teaching a local model to play Pokémon

## Open with the finish screens

Show the four final saves side by side. Each has eight badges, a Hall of Fame entry and 124 required registrations. Then put the external continuation counts under them, 14, 12, 10 and 15. The story is how much autonomy sits between a working finish and one the player can repeat without an operator. [Four-run results](work-sessions/four-run-splits-2026-11-19.md)

## Follow the split clock

Three of the players enter the Hall of Fame at about 2h 57m, within 25 seconds of one another. Fast-forward to 100 registrations. Run 2 is there at 6h 38m. Run 3 arrives at 8h 38m. During the 75-to-100 stretch, Run 3 made 149 acquisition attempts and 142 team restorations. Run 2 made 39 and 31. The elapsed time includes observing menus, traveling and executing those choices. ## Show the smallest failure

Start the solo clip with Squirtle poisoned and the model already heading to heal. The party faints. The game knows what should happen next. The runner stops. On the repaired copy, show the same cartridge state, the blackout fee changing ₽1,265 to ₽632, and the next goal chosen by the model. On the actual solo timeline, leave the restart visible.
