## The research question

Can a local learned player choose useful goals, prepare, fight and recover with
less operator intervention, then carry competence into unfamiliar games? A system
that can execute a supported Red completion is useful infrastructure; it does not
alone answer that question. Semantic state and deterministic skills are disclosed.
The intended runtime uses no LLM calls.

The switch result includes ordinary model-chosen first actions. Earlier controlled
first-action branches are TRAIN lessons. Diagnostic XP and wins are not actual
campaign progress. No global model adoption occurred in the latest block.

## Negative results are part of the result

Psyduck still attacks first, loses the trainee and earns zero trainee XP. Six older
TRAIN control views regress after the switch child changes. A previous trainer
factorial shows that a first-choice label can reverse under a different continuation.
Old switch data mix historical continuation and timing policies; some observable
feature groups have conflicting canonical best labels. These facts do not prove
global infeasibility, but they rule out treating old ranks as universal oracles.

Failed Adam fits, the capped progressing joint trajectory, its separately charged
prefix-verified extension, refused allocations, parser failures and unknown historical
costs remain retained. Audit delays and the earlier missed four-hour review remain
disclosed. A reader repair is engineering work, not learned strategic improvement.

## What a paper still needs

- A precise claim and disclosed assistance/observation/action boundaries.
- Reproducible source, model, environment and evaluation identities, with asset
  licensing and distribution limits respected.
- Preregistered independent evaluation appropriate to the claim, separating
  related snapshots, TRAIN feedback, exposed DEVELOPMENT and held-out starts.
- Baselines/ablations that isolate the contribution being claimed; aggregate
  multi-update comparisons do not isolate one small parameter mask.
- Complete success/failure, intervention, game-work, wall-time and cost reporting,
  including censored attempts and uncertainty. No invented significance or novelty.
- Fresh unchanged Red acceptance and the declared reliability gate before claiming
  full autonomous completion; later-title transfer needs its own evidence.

This is promising research material, not a claim that those conditions have passed.
The next useful test is supplier coverage and nonlethal capture support, followed
by sustained unchanged play. More fitting needs a demonstrated model-owned gap.
See the [audit lessons](project-lessons-2026-10-28.md) for evidence and limits.
It does not require another planner or an expensive replay of the whole story.
