# Research retrospective

November 19, 2026.

Four fresh same-seed players completed the Red route with external continuations. This supplies four complete trajectories for studying cost and recovery. It also gives an unusually clear example of the limits of same-seed replication. All four hit the identical early poison blackout state. A fifth solo run reached it again. One replay beginning before the faint tested all three original choices and the live transition that had previously failed. [Solo recovery](work-sessions/solo-field-blackout-2026-11-19.md)

The group spent 63.3% of its active time after the Hall of Fame. The slower 75-to-100 registration segment took about 90 minutes longer and involved more acquisition and restoration decisions. That finding prioritizes a prospective comparison. It does not identify by itself which alternative the model should choose. [Full split table](work-sessions/four-run-splits-2026-11-19.md)

The next reliability claim needs a single unchanged completion. A broader performance claim needs independent starts, a frozen policy, consistent intervention accounting and comparisons on frames as well as elapsed time.
