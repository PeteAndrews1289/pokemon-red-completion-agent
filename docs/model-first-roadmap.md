actor, attack/switch/replacement execution, decision logs, budgets, retained failures and a
verified natural-loss handoff. Ordinary funding's no-faints verifier is unchanged.

profitable income under J, or model-led fresh-game completion. J is an explicit option, not the

its funding offer cannot silently bypass J's one-to-three-member scope.

## Battle work remaining for the final player

1. **Actual party scope:** qualify up to six own members where required; preserve legal masks
   across fainted reserves and different active slots.
2. **Required move mechanics:** status, recovery, stat changes, Counter, self-destruct and
   all-party Struggle are current exclusions. Add only the mechanics required by the declared
   playing workload, with executor tests and measured training/held-out outcomes.
3. **Goal-aware combat:** battling to win, earning net cash and preserving a capture target are
   different objectives. Do not reuse a trainer knockout policy as a wild capture policy.
4. **Learned recovery and preparation:** the parent chooses healing, training or another goal
   from actual resources. Completing blackout dialogue is not learned strategic recovery.
5. **Representative performance:** report wins/losses, illegal actions, costs and interventions
   against frozen baselines. One loss is valid experience, not grounds for replaying until green.

No perfect-win requirement is introduced. A broader qualification must show useful learning and
valid execution, while retaining randomness and unfavorable outcomes honestly.

## Fresh-run acceptance: all five remain open

1. A genuinely fresh authenticated start, not a stitched development save.
2. Model-directed consequential progression/resource decisions from start to finish.
3. Concurrent Champion-defeated and Hall-of-Fame evidence.
4. All 124 declared native-route registrations in that same run's save.
5. A verified ledger of deferred version, cable-trade, event and mutually exclusive dependencies.

Before launch, the shorter-scenario question must be exhausted, every tested component must pass
its bounded unseen gate, the promotion/rejection decision and costs must be declared, diagnostics
must preserve failure, and the active roadmap must explicitly open the run.
Mew and genuinely unavailable external dependencies remain deferred, not cheated into Red.
A living specimen of every form and level 100 are not requirements.
