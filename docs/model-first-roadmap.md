## The destination and our current position

Four independent starts on one seed each earned eight badges, entered the Hall of Fame and retained all 124 required registrations on their own saves. All four needed external continuations. The new solo run also needed one intervention at the poison blackout. Use full completions to measure reliability, then compare matched candidates on frames and time. ## How a short session earns progress

A session should deliver a reusable failure repair, useful admitted TRAIN evidence, a retained model improvement, or an integrated player milestone. Record which one happened. Gameplay XP, registrations, focused tests and changed weights are different measures.

The F121 session removed a reproduced stopping boundary and demonstrated balls/new registrations on a copy: useful engineering progress, with zero fits. The previous venue rerun reproduced an existing candidate: it added no novel model. Rejected fits can be informative once; repeating the same failed comparison is not progress.

Each work block states one player outcome, the existing components to reuse, the cheapest disproof, an acceptance condition and a finite allowance. Reassess after 15 minutes or two equivalent failures; change tactics rather than repeatedly renaming or replaying the same failure. A negative result must change a decision or provide a specific missing observation.

## The operating loop: whole-player evidence leads the work

The six sections below are workstreams, not six prerequisites to exhaust before play. Use this repeating loop:

**Bounded whole-player rehearsal → rank actual stops and wasted work → repair the top two or three recurring classes and test one useful learning hypothesis → next rehearsal.**

A rehearsal can begin at a relevant retained checkpoint to answer a local integration question. A fresh development campaign measures accumulated integration later; its repairs remain disclosed. No campaign starts under this planning request. Every later launch needs its own finite work order, source and budget; this does not reopen old deadlines.

Do not spend another succession of sessions on speculative edge cases while the integrated player remains unmeasured. Before a third engineering-only block, produce a bounded rehearsal proposal or a concrete hold identifying the unsafe/unverifiable prerequisite, evidence and alternative tactic. If two comparable integration attempts or two useful-fit attempts fail to improve the measured outcome, stop repeating that tactic and record the changed hypothesis in the existing handoff. This limits the patch-only loop; it does not compel an unsafe launch or promise completion in two blocks.

## 1. Reliable execution and recovery

**Player outcome:** continue normal play through battle, travel, supply, storage and observation boundaries without operator repair.

**Already demonstrated:** scoped repairs for route/Fly loops, battle budgets and PP, carrier return, collection handlers, retained outcomes and F121 composite-history settlement. Their exact receipts retain their limits; none proves every boundary is solved.

**Remaining work:** rank technical stops by recurrence, lost useful progress and cost. Do not turn every hypothetical registry/caller-misuse edge case into a launch blocker. Use the retained failure inventory to verify important boundary combinations: battle→field→observation, interrupted task→paired checkpoint→continuation, income→supply→capture, and party/PC/field-carrier return. Preserve ordinary losses as recoverable game outcomes; distinguish a game loss from a technical stop. Resolve known failures with the existing executor/referee before building anything new. Preserve specific provider refusal reasons when a missing action collapses into NO_LEGAL_TARGET, so scarcity is diagnosed rather than hidden by a fallback.

**Exit evidence:** relevant failures reproduce and the repaired paths finish meaningful tasks on copies, with no swallowed errors, unchanged legal actions, paired saves, released controls and full costs. Unknown or unverifiable state fails closed. This does not require predicting every possible game state.

**Reuse:** existing runner, semantic observation adapter, battler, referee, recorder and checkpoint support. Stable stages: `sustained-red`, `red-story`, `red-collection`.

## 2. Purposeful choices: finish objectives, train usefully, keep resources

These are three related workstreams. Diagnose them in parallel where useful, while keeping one source writer and bounded emulator capacity.

| Workstream | What is partly solved | Work still needed | Evidence that closes the gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Goal continuity | Persistent commitment and narrow recurrence escape exist; some whole story objectives passed on copies | Safari/Surf/Koga/Silph and Erika/Fuji switching can still waste fees and continuity; general efficiency is unproved | Matched baseline/candidate whole-objective completion, preserved normal greedy behavior, productive escape from genuine recurrence; no forced goal order or action masks |
| Training and party use | Target/helper custody, trainee return, venue/task accounting and several full TRAIN workloads exist | Choose when training is necessary, whom to train, where, and when to stop; avoid unnecessary carry switching and blanket leveling | Target task completes or justified readiness is met with lower full-job cost across varied trainees/timings, without losing retained useful choices |
| Money, balls and other resources | Normal earning, buying balls and registering species now work on a diagnostic ancestry | Zero/low-cash recovery and partial coin spending can still derail collection; spending quantity/reserve is partly deterministic | Earn→buy→register chain under scarcity, truthful alternatives/costs and useful purchase quantities; no asset liquidation default or invented income |

For training, count target XP separately from helper XP and include travel, healing, PP, injuries, storage and return costs. More XP against a stronger opponent is not automatically the cheapest completed task. Multiple Fearows are a party-role/coverage/cost question, not a reason to hardcode species diversity.

The saved 21, 000-coin audit is complete: costs are represented, but clipping and out-of-range storage features confound predictions. One economy-only fit was held after native comparison. The next resource question is useful whole-task cost, not hiding that spend. Previously, the immediate diagnostic was: The model selected the coin goal; support selected the quantity with a zero reserve. Its EconomyOffer disclosed the spend, while portable `resource_cost` was zero. Reproduce the relevant scorer inputs before deciding whether the defect is representation, quantity support or learned ranking.

**Reuse:** current goal/sequence/readiness/venue heads, movement/cycle memory, sampler, supply and party skills. Stable stages: `adaptive-red`, `red-story`, `red-collection`. A copied improvement is not independent generalization.

### Specific learning questions already identified

The prior audit reported ordinary preparation missing its story-purpose join (`story_objective_ref`), short three/four-decision windows that may omit task payoff, and remaining route cost unavailable to the parent comparison. Check these semantic/credit links before adding features, capacity or another fit. They are diagnosis hypotheses, not proof that the model representation is impossible. The old F97 Lance loop is a historical regression case, not proof the current F121 build still has that stop.

## 3. Turn useful comparisons into retained learning

**Player outcome:** better choices survive integration into the normal runner.

Do not wait for every execution bug to disappear before improving policy. Learning and reliability can advance together; each learning task still needs trustworthy outcomes. DEVELOPMENT runs diagnose questions; they are never relabeled as TRAIN examples.

1. Name one consequential contrast and the new information before proposing a fit: stay/switch, prepare/attempt/stop, target/venue, or spend/save. The next named learning question is whether preparation is linked to the actual story task and whether its benefit survives the observation horizon.
2. Use eligible prospective TRAIN origins and the same selector, task contract, horizon, feature and reward recipe intended for production. Retain failed/censored outcomes and unknown costs honestly.
3. Reuse saved complete workloads before recollecting. New collection may target learner-visited TRAIN situations prospectively admitted through the existing contract; merely tagging segments of an exposed DEVELOPMENT campaign cannot make them TRAIN.  A single timing or many sibling snapshots does not become many independent origins.
4. Fit an existing component only when there is useful information. Keep baseline/rehearsal examples, preserve older justified choices and separate response-policy families.
5. Compare on held-out compatible tasks, then native whole-task benefit. A better training score with unchanged native behavior is not an adopted improvement.

The venue record illustrates the gate: one fit improved TRAIN ranking but regressed Diglett cost by 6.47%; it was rejected. Joint rehearsal later preserved scores, but two native comparisons had identical outcomes/costs. The latest repeat exactly reproduced that existing candidate; do not repeat that fit again without a new informative contrast.

**Exit evidence:** an admitted dataset, a genuinely changed candidate, retained prior competence and useful held-out/native improvement on the declared comparison. Keep baseline if the candidate fails. No new planner, battler or training-root factory is implied. Stable stages: `foundation`, `adaptive-red`, `sustained-red`.

## 4. Integrate one recipe and measure useful throughput

**Player outcome:** the repaired skills and improved choices work together at a practical cost.

Freeze the chosen source, model, flags, target=124 contract and assistance for each comparison. Reuse original55/MID/LATE where their existing contracts answer the question, plus relevant retained failure copies. Preserve their exact denominators; passing another build's story-only gate does not qualify the current full-124 recipe.

Acceptance is the declared whole-objective/collection outcome, reliable continuation, and complete cost—not many menus or minutes played. Do not extend an unproductive probe merely to collect tiny gains. If an old test no longer measures a current failure, explain that rather than adding a new ceremonial gate.

Profile observation/menu construction, startup verification, routing, battle execution and log growth separately. Reduce the dominant measured cost while preserving legal menus, quotes and guards. Emulation is already uncapped; faster emulation or fewer dashboard snapshots does not by itself remove selector/observation overhead. Compare useful objectives/registrations per wall-clock time at matched outcome quality.

Start with one worker. Use two only when the existing memory guards and measured aggregate throughput justify it. Four-worker population search is optional later, not a prerequisite for the first completion. Seeds are emulator timing/RNG schedules, not independent origins merely because filenames differ.

**Exit evidence:** one exact integrated recipe passes the relevant bounded task comparisons and has a measured campaign runtime/resource budget. This is readiness evidence, not the final run. Stable stages: `sustained-red`, `red-story`, `red-collection`.

## 5. Deliver a complete run, then demonstrate repeatability

| Milestone | Purpose | What counts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh DEVELOPMENT rehearsal | Find remaining integration failures without pretending development is frozen evaluation | Same-save progress with every repair, restore, loss and cost retained; repairs may continue under the user's finite work order, but the run keeps its assisted/mixed-build label |
| First unchanged fresh completion | Meet the first delivery | Authenticated clean power-on; one frozen source/model/config; Champion/HOF plus 124 in the same save; zero operator fixes, restore/rollback, teacher fallback or runtime LLM; all attempts retained |
| Held-out reliability series | Establish repeatability after the first success | Prospectively declared schedules and limits; initial contractual target remains at least 8 successes in 10 held-out clean starts; all failures count |

**When to cut a frozen candidate:** the chosen recipe's fresh start and target124 binding are checked; known stopping classes from the latest rehearsal have relevant successful replays; and coherent whole-task continuations cover story, necessary preparation and late collection/resource recovery. Declare remaining risks, support, costs and the exact recipe, then make a release decision. This is not a requirement to prove every imaginable state or to finish another already-frozen official run first.

**After a failed frozen attempt:** retain it as a failure, diagnose its terminal on copies, and return to development. A repaired continuation never edits the frozen attempt into a pass. A later frozen attempt needs its own prospective launch identity and denominator; do not pool successful portions across builds.

An ordinary development campaign can be authorized after relevant safety checks without waiting for research-grade proof of every subsystem. Formal frozen acceptance has the stricter contract above. This roadmap launches neither: campaigns remain stopped until a new finite launch request.

Successive generations/population selection become useful when the score rewards actual completion and complete costs, preserves failures and has trustworthy recovery/termination. A fast partial run must not beat a slower complete one through censored costs or a broken reward. Reuse existing learners before adding a population system.

Do not promise a completion date from the number of bug fixes. Re-estimate after integrated whole-objective results, then the first full rehearsal; report likely remaining experiment blocks and the uncertainty, not a calendar estimate from checklist percentages.
