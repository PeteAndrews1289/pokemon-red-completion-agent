## Claude: statistical integrity and claim boundaries

### P0 — clustering had no numeric power certificate

**Finding:** accepted. The legacy test obtains power from 105 independent Bernoulli contexts, while
the clustered pivot permits multiple related scenarios per upstream lineage. The earlier roadmap
required a future cluster-aware design but did not bind a numeric independent-cluster minimum or
correlation sensitivity.

**Evidence checked:** `living_dex_causal_curriculum.py` hard-codes the V1 105-context paired design;
`living_dex_causal_capacity_schedule.py` demands 195 distinct roots; and
`living_dex_clustered_curriculum.py` allows bounded siblings with equal total lineage weight.
Running the dependency-free prospective design function over the declared 0.30 win, 0.10 loss,
0.05 alpha, and 0.80 power alternative gives **67 independent evaluable units without censoring**.
With three forced independent-unit losses, the minimum is **100**.

**Decision:** the R1 design must publish `K_min`, use independent lineage units for its binding
certificate, report correlation sensitivity through the worst case of 1.0, and bar the 105 raw-row
object from certifying clustered power. A different effect or test is allowed only prospectively
and needs its own numeric calculation. No powered outcomes may open before that design exists.

### P1 — Crystal controls were weaker than Red controls

**Finding:** accepted. A zero-initialized identical architecture isolates the value of Red-trained
weights, while the full Red best-of-three envelope prevents a weak heuristic from making transfer
look impressive. One without the other leaves an avoidable ambiguity.

**Decision:** the Crystal zero-shot protocol must beat both the exact R2 best-of-three envelope and
zero initialization on identical frozen contexts. The former controls heuristic-reachable
structure; the latter controls shared architecture and projection.

### P2 — replacement evaluation had no lineage budget

**Finding:** accepted. Requiring a fresh development partition after every failed candidate is the
right leakage rule, but without a finite supply and candidate cap it creates pressure to reuse an
opened exam or generate roots indefinitely.

**Decision:** before development opens, freeze total unused independent-lineage supply, its
train/development/contingency allocation, and the maximum candidate count
`floor(L_development / K_min)`. Exhaustion ends the lane; it never permits reuse.

## Antigravity: hierarchy and cross-title transfer

### P0 — historical Crystal V3 conflated supported failure and correct abstention

**Finding:** accepted; proposed repair rejected. The unopened historical protocol globally sets
`missing_prediction_is_incorrect`. Changing that flag globally to false would reward a model for
refusing every supported decision.

**Decision:** retire the old goal-manager V3 protocol unopened for this living-Dex bundle. Its
replacement has two prospectively assigned endpoints: missing prediction or abstention is failure
on shared supported mechanics; typed abstention is correct on declared unsupported mechanics, and
attempting those mechanics is failure. Unknown capability state is reported separately.

### P1 — the Crystal capability vocabulary omits important Gen-II boundaries

**Finding:** accepted. The current enum represents happiness, time, breeding, roaming, and generic
puzzle interaction, but lacks typed gender requirements, held-item workflow, phone events, weekly
events, and renewable berry acquisition. Without those types, a prospect cannot reliably fail
closed.

**Decision:** R5 compatibility now requires those capability types before a Crystal context can be
classified. Implementing them belongs to the bounded R5 adapter qualification, not the immediate
eight-row Red fit.

### P2 — the executable 124-target Red acquisition graph is not the full mission denominator

**Finding:** modified. Antigravity correctly identified the product gap but overstated the current
contract. `RED_SOLO_COLLECTION_CONTRACT` already partitions all 151 species into 124 single-save
registration targets and 27 typed exclusions; the acquisition graph deliberately covers the
executable 124-target subplan. Forcing external blockers into `RedAcquisitionMethod` would mix
executable mechanics with unavailable dependencies.

**Decision:** preserve the tested solo subgraph and build a 151-target completion catalog above it.
The other targets become explicit version, trade, second-save, mutually exclusive, or legitimate-
event dependencies. Nonrepeatable static encounters, gifts, fossils, and branch evolutions carry
one-shot inventory and irreversible-risk facts. The 124/120 solo result cannot graduate as a full
living Pokedex.

Neither reviewer found a reason to abandon the hierarchical model-first direction. Their six
findings were specification gaps at R1, R2, R4, and R5; none requires delaying the current R0
integration-fit engineering. The immediate board remains **causal Red train 8/60 · powered fit 0 ·
