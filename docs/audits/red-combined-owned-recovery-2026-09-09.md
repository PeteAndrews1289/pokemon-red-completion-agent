forced story step. It passed Pidgeot, Alakazam and Rhydon, but stopped at Gyarados

## Six-part mission check

1. Capability: one recovery goal can retain both existing owned-item skills.
2. Authority: native83 chooses recovery versus story repeatedly; sub-item
   selection stays explicitly deterministic, not a learned medicine policy.
3. Transfer: resource/HP/PP permutations and missing/unsafe-state tests, then a
   newly declared correlated continuation from the successful Lance checkpoint.
4. Falsifier: both recovery needs remain accessible in a three-decision menu.
5. Time box: one composition patch and bounded outcome, then reassess.
6. Stop: any lost specimen, unverified spend, unsupported state or execution
   failure. No reset, retry of the closed Champion plan, or failed-save resume.

The new prospective mode tries the existing affordable HP/status provider with
its conditional last-Full-Restore reserve, then the existing PP provider only when no HP
target/resource is available. It does not fall back after an execution failure,
alter targets, execute either provider during observation, or refill the bag.
The native model already fitted the single admitted Lance outcome:83 total
outcomes, all prior rows retained. No part of the failed Champion trace is fitted.

The reserve is not absolute: below-half HP or a status condition may spend the
last Full Restore. A literal endpoint-HP test verifies Jolteon is the first
target and, after that heal, PP restoration remains available while the final
Full Restore is retained. Internal read-only review found no blocking defect;
its request to qualify and disclose this conditional behavior is incorporated.

Validation:406 focused ROM-free tests pass across composition, profile/factory,
