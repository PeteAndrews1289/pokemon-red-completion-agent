## What this does not establish

The head's clipped completion predictions of one for healing and zero for story
are not calibrated confidence. They come from only three returns at one initial
resource context, including one controller-support failure rather than a defeat.
The frozen continuation sampled story with probability **0.3233**, while assigning
restoration **0.6767**. Therefore the observed victory validates this realized
continuation, not reliable continuation or a measured advantage over an unplayed
alternative. Two heals can still consume the two-macro budget without the goal.

Bruno and Agatha now each have a fitted finite-goal head that has made an actual
first choice in a changed-resource training probe. They are separate heads, not
a recursively learned whole-game player. Phase4 still requires model-directed
completion with concurrent Champion and Hall-of-Fame evidence under declared
authority. That exit has not been met.
