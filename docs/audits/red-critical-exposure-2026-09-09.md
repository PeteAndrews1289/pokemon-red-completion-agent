# Bounded critical exposure: engineering reorientation

## Why change the support policy?

The retained Lance continuation has defeated Gyarados, both Dragonair and Aerodactyl.
It stops at Dragonite with all six members alive. The last Full Restore was actually
spent; no item is refunded. Current Blastoise HP164 exceeds its supported ordinary
incoming upper104 but not the critical-inclusive upper182. The old zero-critical-risk
policy cannot proceed. This is not evidence that ordinary play is impossible.

The living-Pokédex ledger represents retained ownership, not current HP. Fainting
is reversible but still costs resources and can produce failure. Nevertheless, the
old zero-faint controller contract remains unchanged: a new opt-in actor explicitly
accepts critical-hit exposure and stops on the first faint. No automatic Revive,
forced replacement, blackout recovery, sacrifice, replay or probability claim.

## Exact bounded authority

The new actor first asks the unchanged strict controller. Only its typed
no-supported-survival-action result permits fallback. Unknown mechanics do not.
Ordinary and critical-inclusive bounds come from the same fresh observation and
retain confusion/residual allowances. Only the active member's supported attack
can use the risk allowance; switching remains strict and no items are authorized.

At most two durable attack intents across the failed-recovery ancestry are allowed.
An intent is claimed before input, not inferred from PP consumption. A confusion
self-hit can consume a turn without PP; the earlier Aerodactyl trace demonstrates
that distinction. Missing durable recording prevents input; interrupted attempts
are not refunded. The game runtime latches the selected move during menu control.
A per-macro guard blocks further input on active HP zero even before party HP sync,
including dialogue that precedes the next MAIN-menu guard.

The read-only exact-state qualification chose a risk attack with zero frames and
inputs.108 focused tests pass; a larger related suite is also run before publication.
Independent review found no blocker and requested a zero-risk intermediate ancestry
test, now included. This is not a successful Lance result or a learned battle policy.

story/resource/party options, then admitted outcomes and a subsequent evaluation.
