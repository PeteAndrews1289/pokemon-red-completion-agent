# Native story/restoration choice integration

## Mission check — before implementation

1. **Capability:** choose between advancing a supported story objective and spending
   an owned item on a damaged party member; reuse observed cartridge routing and
   existing field-item execution.
2. **Learned authority:** expose optional restoration below the emergency threshold
   alongside an executable next-story option. Emergency recovery remains deterministic.
3. **Transfer test:** varied ROM-free party, inventory, safety-pressure, and trainer
   objective cases; the retained Red continuation is same-lineage integration, not
   independent transfer or generalization.
4. **Cheapest falsifier:** a zero-input observation of post-Lorelei checkpoint93c7
   must expose two supported alternatives without inventing resources or completed
   actions. Failures remain outcomes, never fabricated successes.
5. **Time box:** one integration session, at most two hours before reassessment.
6. **Stop condition:** stop before gameplay if the genuine menu cannot be qualified;
   otherwise retain one selected outcome and assess existing fit eligibility. No
   Lorelei replay, full-game replay, or broader battle-mechanics expansion.

## Starting evidence

Completed Lorelei checkpoint93c7 retains6163currency and three Full Restores.
The native story adapter currently supports only the already-completed Lorelei
objective. Legacy field restoration requires sufficient prescribed items for all
damaged members; it cannot use the current inventory. The V3 scorer recognizes a
restoration option, but the safety shell also forces non-emergency restoration
whenever the teacher prefers it. These are integration gaps, not learning results.

Model76 remains unchanged pending a verified new learned outcome.
