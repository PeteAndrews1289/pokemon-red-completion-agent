## Reusable engineering

`red_owned_evolution_inventory.py` now derives one prerequisite row per missing
level-evolution target from an observed collection and cartridge rules. It keeps
duplicate-acquisition deficits visible, distinguishes registration from living
ownership, retains private specimen locations, and does not mistake multiple
copies for multiple objectives. Stone and trade requirements remain outside this
level-only inventory. No species or slot identifiers become policy features.

This is an action-free inventory, **not an execution-readiness proof or connected
model selector**. Surplus ownership alone says nothing about routes, safe battles
or storage operations. Branches share precursor stock; rows are not a simultaneous
allocation. The module was run against X's audited inventory without an emulator.
