# Red battle contrast curriculum V2

Status: engineering implementation only. No outcome, prediction, model fit, controller input,
sealed Red case, Crystal context, replay, or authority is authorized by this document.

## Why this is a new policy

Clustered V1 required every one of five fresh train contexts to expose at least three usable
actions. Its exact action-free run found counts `2, 3, 2, 3, 3`, so V1 failed and remains failed.
V2 does not edit that rule or reinterpret the V1 result. It has a separate schema and policy hash.

The new question is appropriate for a small descriptive integration update: does the fixed roster
span enough distinct *within-context action contrasts* to exercise the learner without allowing a
context with more actions to count as more independent gameplay evidence?

## Frozen information unit

- One upstream capture is one independent example and receives equal fit weight.
- Every supported action is measured from that capture's exact reset.
- With `k` usable actions, the information inventory contains `k - 1` action-hidden contrasts
  against the first canonical action. These are directions inside one example, not `k - 1`
  independent episodes.
- Every train and development context must expose at least two actions.
- At least three of five fresh train contexts and six of eight development contexts must expose
  three or more actions.
- The resulting minimum inventories are nine train contrast rows and fourteen development contrast
  rows. The observed hidden matrices must attain full available row rank up to width fourteen and
  clear the existing singular-value floor.
- Venue, prior-margin, party-condition, level-gap, lineage, root, state, observation, prior-model,
  runtime, catalog, and claim-availability gates remain unchanged.

The nine/fourteen minima follow from the declared context counts and multiway requirements; they
are not claims of nine/fourteen independent episodes. The eventual dataset still contains six
train examples—one retained and five fresh—and eight development examples.

## What a pass would permit

A pass permits one bounded full-action outcome collection and one train-only descriptive fit.
Development choices must be committed before development outcomes are opened. The comparison is
paired and descriptive because eight held contexts cannot establish broad statistical competence.
The updated model remains shadow-only.

A pass does not permit learned battle authority, autonomous navigation, a full Red replay, sealed
Red evaluation, Crystal execution, or a living-Pokédex claim. Those require materially larger
independent curricula and separate promotion gates.

## Execution order

1. Publish this policy and require green CI on the exact main commit.
2. Run the V2 action-free qualifier exactly once against the already authenticated fixed catalogs.
3. Stop and publish either its qualification or its terminal rejection.
4. Only a qualification may open a separately reoriented outcome-collection session.

No V1 retry, outcome-based row selection, cloned capture, route patch, or threshold reduction is
allowed.
