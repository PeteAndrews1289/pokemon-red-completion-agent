## Mission fit

The product is a transferable agent that completes stories and builds living Pokedexes across
games. Red is the first curriculum and Crystal is the first transfer test. This gate does not teach
the model or improve game completion by itself; it makes seven retained Red battle decisions safe
to collect without reviving an exhausted root or teaching a route.

The preceding V7 census proved that ten unattempted, claim-available roots can each support a real
two-action battle decision at both Route 11 and Diglett's Cave. V2 turns that aggregate fact into an
immutable private denominator while keeping public evidence path-free and identity-free.

## Implemented contract

- Preserve the historical V1 plan and its consumed seven-root journal unchanged.
- Open the complete successor state bank under the shared account-wide claim lease.
- Reopen the exact exhausted V1 plan and terminal journal and exclude every attempted source.
- Recompute every eligible root-to-venue edge with the same reachable-venue and mechanics rules as
  the action-free census.
- Freeze the complete eligible inventory, then use the title-neutral exact allocator to select
  seven unique roots across at least two venues with at most six at any venue.
- Bind the selected venue, measured level band, source boundary, mechanics-supported living party
  slot, ROM, source bundle, capture destination, exhausted-evidence hashes, and output filenames.
- Reopen the canonical private plan at zero frames and actions. Serialized assignments are checked
  by recomputing the allocation and party-slot choice.
- Before execution, the runner independently reopens the exhausted evidence and rejects any reused
  source. It durably marks an assignment started before launching the controller-capable child.
- The child rederives the plan-bound reachable venue from the loaded state before controller input.
  It accepts no caller-supplied partition, lineage, source location, or arrow sequence.
- Route 11 and Diglett's Cave relocation reuse the existing bounded Red transition callbacks.
  Party identity must survive relocation.
- A started or failed assignment never returns to pending. A restart can authenticate already
  complete outputs, but cannot retry controller input.
