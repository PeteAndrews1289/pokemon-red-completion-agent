# Red multi-goal training session plan

## Mission

The product is a transferable player that can finish Pokémon games and build a living Pokédex.
Red is the first curriculum. The model should choose semantic objectives; deterministic skills
continue to handle controller-level mechanics, verification and safety.

## What the inventory changed

The retained bank contains 81 authenticated, byte-distinct Red states: 54 nominal train and 27
nominal development, with declared providers spanning all nine goal families. That makes the bank
useful for engineering and calibration. It does **not** prove 81 independent experiences. The old
artifacts did not retain a trustworthy upstream-lineage mapping, so the nominal development split
cannot yet support an unseen-generalization claim.

This distinction no longer blocks all useful learning. It changes the label on the evidence:

- existing open train roots may produce **calibration training**;
- existing development roots may provide **same-bank diagnostics**;
- authority promotion and Crystal transfer still require new prospectively partitioned roots.

## Execution sequence

1. **Complete — action-free inventory.** Authenticate all context bytes, profiles and partitions;
   reject paths, predictions, outcomes and teacher-choice fields; require explicit lineage evidence
   before calling a split independent.
2. **Freeze a compact calibration plan.** Select still-unconsumed train roots mechanically, before
   outcomes or model scores, across team development, evolution and storage. Claim one physical
   root once and schedule all supported strategic candidates from that reset where practical.
3. **Collect bounded Red outcomes.** Restore the exact state for each candidate, execute one
   semantic goal through the deterministic skill layer, and retain success, typed failure, ledger
   delta, actions, frames and resource cost. No teacher chooses the answer.
4. **Fit the semantic goal model.** Train only on completed calibration outcomes. Keep identity,
   coordinates, route strings and teacher selections out of model features.
5. **Run same-bank diagnostics.** Commit predictions first, then execute untouched diagnostic
   roots. Report this as local robustness, not independent generalization.
6. **Exercise multi-decision play.** Let the fitted manager choose two to four consecutive goals,
   reobserve after each skill and recover through typed failures while the living-Pokédex ledger
   verifies progress.
7. **Build the independent gate in parallel with evidence, not ceremony.** Before promotion or
   Crystal, obtain at least eight prospective train and four development lineages, disjoint by
   upstream episode and physical root, covering at least four and three strategic families.

## Immediate stop rules

- Never reuse consumed pairs 001–003 or any already claimed physical root.
- Do not select contexts because the current model disagrees with a baseline or because their
  outcomes are convenient.
- Do not call same-bank diagnostics held out, independent or transferable.
- Do not replace a failed semantic skill with a route-specific button script.
- Keep recovery, restoration and collection-safety overrides deterministic until learned behavior
  has outcome evidence strong enough to replace them.

## Expected cadence

The action-free inventory is complete and its repository checkpoint passed 6,559 non-integration
tests, Ruff, mypy, generated-registry reproduction, and public/documentation/product-focus gates.
The next engineering session should freeze and qualify the compact calibration campaign. Once that
runner is green, the first new outcome batch and model fit should take one additional work session;
useful multi-goal comparisons should follow within one to two further sessions. Full Red autonomy
and living-Pokédex completion remain later milestones, not claims attached to this pilot.
