## Six-part mission check

1. Capability: reserve selection and stay/switch choices after faints and attrition.
2. Learned authority: only switch/control weights; damage/status and masks fixed.
3. Transfer: 24 fresh constructed teams, pair-disjoint from authenticated TRAIN pairs;
   deployed, parent and correction actors over three timings. Shared roots remain
   correlated; this is not independent natural-game or cross-title qualification.
4. Cheapest falsifier: zero-input setup checks, then two measured native lessons.
5. Time box: two hours, ending 16:58 UTC; at most one correction fitting procedure.
6. Stop: censored/illegal episode, inadequate supply, inseparable measured choices,
   retention failure or failed comparison. No automatic successor or campaign input.

Reuse the six status-attrition and six forced-replacement TRAIN input teams, not
comparison teams. Their candidate-continuation trajectories use new identities
and select the first declared phase after faints, without outcome filtering.
Record reused lineage and any repeated TRAIN state; neither is independent evidence.

Status-family inputs select the first MAIN boundary after at least one faint with
two living members. Forced-family inputs select the first forced replacement with
two legal alternatives after two faints for six-member teams, one for three-member
teams. Keep every outcome and every completed target, including losses.

Measure every legal first action at five timings using the frozen experimental
parent. Branch history resets identically across alternatives. Pilot two contexts;
then require at least eight targets, including three of each phase. Check that
measured return differences are distinguishable in the current feature vectors.

Preserve all old groups and the complete 36-target corpus, both jointly and by
family. Fit switch then rebuilt composed control targets once: 2400 epochs each,
rate 0.005, pairwise regret. Require at least 10% lower full composed control regret,
nonincreasing switch regret, no retained-group regression beyond 1e-10, and fixed
damage/status components. Do not drop the proposal gap from regret accounting.

Only a passed fit opens 216 fresh comparison episodes. Against both deployed and
experimental parent actors, require noninferior wins, noninferior common-win PP
and decisions, improved combined risk rate, and zero illegal actions/interventions.
No perfect-win requirement. No automatic deployment even if all gates pass.

5000 actions, 240000 frames, 45 seconds, 160 decisions and 80 player turns.
