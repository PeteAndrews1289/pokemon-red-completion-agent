# Portfolio brief

## Thirty-second explanation

I direct an AI-assisted project to build a Pokémon player that learns useful goals
and battle decisions, verifies outcomes and eventually transfers competence between
games. Small learned models work with deterministic control skills and semantic
emulator observations. This is active development, not a finished autonomous or
pixel-only player.

## Demonstrated results

The current earned fresh-player run completes Red's story,Champion/Hall of Fame
and all124 declared registrations on its own save. Its last block adds73 entries
from51:70 during model-owned goals,three singleton support. All four legendary
specimens and six original individuals are retained. Earlier completed lineages
remain separate; no progress is merged. All workers are stopped for review.

The reusable foundation includes native battle practice, fitted move/switching
models, semantic goal selection, navigation, capture, evolution, storage, ordinary
economy, shared registration memory and retained failure/cost records.

These results do not establish frozen unassisted autonomy. Mid-run execution
repairs and disclosed medicine,capture and PC mechanics remain assistance.
The assembled player's models/game assets are private; current gameplay reuses
weights rather than constituting new parameter training.

## Immediate work and final goal

Review the completed run's244 outcomes and decision,battle,recovery,resource and
timing journals. Separate learned choice from execution/support,then choose the
smallest freeze-readiness work order. Do not replay Red merely to recount coverage
or rebuild the existing battler without a demonstrated learning gap.

The first final gate remains one fresh model-directed Red story and 124 legitimate
same-save registrations. A compatible unfamiliar hack, Crystal and at least
Emerald follow. Blue or later legitimate titles can add deferred dependencies.
There is no demonstrated cross-title transfer or defensible overall completion
percentage yet.

## Authorship and evidence

Pete Andrews defines requirements, directs development and validates outcomes.
Codex, Claude and Antigravity have contributed code and reviews. The project is
explicitly AI-assisted; coding assistants are not secretly selecting live actions.
Python, PyBoy, NumPy, SQLite, typed skills and automated tests support the system.
