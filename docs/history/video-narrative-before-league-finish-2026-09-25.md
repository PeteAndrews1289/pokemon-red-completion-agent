An AI-assisted engineering project directed by Pete Andrews.
Edition: September25,2026UTC. The story spans July28–September25, not only the latest gym.
[Full chronicle](../chronicle/README.md) · [Narration draft](../chronicle/video-script-through-2026-09-22.md).

## Editorial premise

We built software that could finish Red before we had built a model that could
really play it. The series follows the difference: authority, consequences,
failures, learning and the engineering needed to connect them.

Do not open with “we built an autonomous Pokémon master.” The earned story still
has eight badges; the final fresh-run criteria remain unpassed.
The spinning floor tile is a present-day hook, not the entire project history.

## Visual treatment

Use a persistent authority legend: learned choice / support execution / verification.
Show separate collection and story saves; never animate them as one continuous run.
A timeline should keep failed attempts and rejected models visible rather than
cut straight from implementation to success.

Candidate visuals include source excerpts, game-state summaries, decision/event
logs, resource changes and diagrams of the route's predicted and observed endpoint.
Use gameplay footage only when a corresponding recording exists and its origin is
known. Label diagrams and recreated scenes as reconstructions.
A save or receipt is not proof that a video was recorded.

## Claims that need explicit qualification on screen

- July/August route completions are not final autonomous fresh-Red acceptance.
- Most independent units are origins or episodes, not every turn or timing variant.
- Teacher-edited training setups are allowed; final-player save edits are not.
- XP gained by Pokémon is different from fitting model weights.
- Lorelei fell on an earned model-selected retry in53battle choices; the new
  loss-aware strategy policy is still untrained. Four faints and exhausted Surf
  remain real costs, not evidence of whole-League readiness.
- Owned-item recovery then enabled an earned Bruno win with both usable members
  surviving. The recovery target was supplied, not learned. Copy and actual
  execution reproduced one deterministic trajectory, not two independent wins.
- A successful battle with a faint can still fail a stricter funding contract.
- The604wild wins earned298302Pokémon XP, not new model weights. Prepared Lorelei
  and Bruno wins led to another Agatha copy loss. Different rosters/resources and
  timing prevent a causal claim that grinding made the policy worse. Seven
  zero-coverage reserve choices and nine immune attacks motivate a measured
  switch/control lesson; those risk counts do not prove an alternative victory.
- The additive status actor failed its original efficiency screen; owner-directed
  development use is a disclosed exception.
- Flash and Claude contributions were real but mixed; usage savings were not measured.
- Earlier Crystal adapter probes are not demonstrated learned cross-title transfer.
- No novelty, paper acceptance or overall-project completion percentage is established.
- The September23 result is41/48exact-best damage choices in a one-turn comparison;
  it is not41battle wins. The subsequent full-battle comparison separately measured
 38/72wins versus18/72, with lower common-win turns/PP. That constructed TRAIN
 stress bank is not a final story win-rate estimate. Dig/Fly compatibility later
 passed64episodes; both actors6/24full-battle wins. Native healing exposed a PP
 accounting issue: composition actual common-win spending fell18.6%, while the
 earlier20.3%was endpoint depletion. The candidate then won the actual Route22
 rival in26audited choices and recovered; this is not a paired causal comparison.
 No battle was replayed; see the chronicle.
