## Mission check

- Capability:learn delayed useful progress with the actual cost of recovery.
- Authority:prepare a separately versioned target,without changing live choices.
- Transfer:counts and resource costs contain no quest,map or trainer identities.
- Falsifier:an incomplete horizon or unverified counter cannot become a known zero;

- Time box:one target/reconstruction packet,then reassess the native supply and
  existing audit path before adding a fitter or collecting more records.
- Stop:unverifiable source/selected action,post-choice features,leaked evaluation
  rows,or the need to invent first-visit differences between unknown branches.

## Fixed semantics,version1

Use seven separate outcomes over the declared1–4sampled-choice horizon:
unique native progress,completed quests,controller actions,frames,observed cash
debits,observed cash credits,and losses with complete native battle coverage.
The last channel is deliberately called battle losses,not independently proven
blackouts. Top-level reports alone do not establish it. Cash is the sum of net
changes at recorded goal boundaries,not gross purchases/income.

Each channel is normalized by n/(n+scale),with scales1,1,1000,60000,1000,1000,1.
Keep raw totals and known/unknown masks. Zero is a target only when the entire
window and that channel are independently verified. An interrupted prefix is
a lower-bound diagnostic only:all full-horizon target channels are unknown.
Do not impute zero,drop its original receipts or reuse it as a completed window.
No immediate action-success reward or bootstrapped unobserved tail is included.
The scale constants must be fixed before a new experiment,not tuned on comparison.

## Learning and evaluation consequence

Keep legacy policy and economy weights frozen. The separate head eventually fits
only authenticated selected-action TRAIN windows under one declared continuation
policy and horizon. Use channel masks,not zero-filled missing labels;report sample
counts per channel. Predictions without sufficient known targets stay unavailable.
Do not interpret these on-policy continuations as counterfactual branch outcomes.

Unknown first-visit branches may still alias. History can support learned
reselection after experience,not an omniscient initial preference. Before another
native trial,require a meaningful input contrast and a useful target contrast,
then declare new TRAIN roots,independent comparison roots,limits,fit identity and
adoption rule. No new fitting implementation is needed until admission is resolved.
