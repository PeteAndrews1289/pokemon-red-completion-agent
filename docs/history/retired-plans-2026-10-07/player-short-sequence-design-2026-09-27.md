## Mission check and scope

- Capability: distinguish useful progress across several decisions from successful
  execution of the next movement;retain the entire cost of setbacks and recovery.
- Learned authority: eventually predict measured continuation outcomes for the
  selected branch or preparation action. This packet changes no live authority.
- Transfer test: use title-neutral observations;rename/reorder private branches
  without changing features or outcome accounting. Unknown future stays unknown.
- Cheapest falsifier: equal immediate actions with different known later challenges
  must differ;unknown branches must not acquire invented differences. A loss then
  healing must retain its loss and spending despite the healthy terminal state.
- Time box:60–90minutes,then reassess before implementation in the live learner.
- Stop condition: feature distinctions require quest identity or future truth;
  accounting cannot preserve actual ordered outcomes;or the proposal needs an
  unbounded search,teacher-selected branch order or rewriting historical targets.

## Reuse and the actual missing connection

Reuse native skill bindings,model-owned menus,FrozenK,budget accounting,provenance
and the existing selected-option outcome fitter. That fitter consumes one measured
outcome per selected option;its bootstrap helpers extend feature dimensions,not
temporal credit. Historical V1–V6 vectors and targets must remain unchanged.

Two additions need separate proofs:

1. A prospective bounded sequence preview carries known later challenges,with
   explicit unknowns. A preview is information,not a commanded action sequence.
   The current OptionChallenge schema is reusable. No branch name,map,event ID or
   post-choice result belongs in the feature vector. Native sourcing of later
   challenges remains to be designed;the prototype cannot certify knowledge.
2. A measured fixed-horizon continuation outcome preserves first-choice identity,
   state continuity,elapsed actions/frames,observed cash changes,blackouts and unique
   progress. Immediate action success is not whole-quest completion. A truncated
   or interrupted prefix is not a complete horizon label. No unselected labels.

The continuation is conditional on its recorded behavior policy,not a causal
claim that the first choice caused every later gain. Later model choices remain
free;the preview cannot force commitment. Use nonoverlapping prospective windows
initially;do not treat overlapping suffixes or same-origin copies as independent.
Avoid discounted sums of the old success label:they would reward repeated cheap
travel and healing,and could count the same unlock repeatedly.
