## Findings that determine the work

1. **Resource completion needs its own contract.** `NativePlayerCurriculum`
   checks story objectives, specimen levels and restoration. The observer uses
   that contract when no offers remain. Reusing it for acquisition could report
   completion despite unmet resource requirements. This is a missing extension,
   not a demonstrated false result in the already-declared story curriculum.
2. **A useful purchase is currently hidden.** The retained source has 3175 cash
   and no balls/healing items. The legacy minimum-of-two-reserves score rejects
   buying balls alone. Existing `capture_reserve_purchase` already derives an
   affordable five-ball/1000 order. It still needs route and clerk qualification.
3. **The exact order must survive every seam.** The router constructs a provider
   for routing/destination binding, and `_with_quotes` constructs one again.
   A local override is insufficient if pricing or arrival recreates the original
   provider. Preserve one declared order through availability, quote, travel and
   fresh arrival checks; reject stale facts rather than quietly increasing it.
4. **Available offers and actor observations must agree.** The continuous observer
   supplies the original registered situation to the actor. Prove meaningful
   shortage/stock/price distinctions survive its semantic projection, not merely
   that an executable purchase was inserted into the menu. No map IDs or route
   identities in policy features; do not casually introduce another feature version.
5. **Income is conditional, not universally ready.** Existing finite trainer
   funding requires a prepared lead at least ten levels above the opposing top
   level. The level-8 source cannot be assumed eligible. This conservative legacy
   restriction is a future strategic limitation, not a reason to grind this
   opening source solely to manufacture a test. Ordinary story payouts are distinct.
   Exact-reserve purchasing must not destroy the original affordable-provider
   contract used by the income planner. No default item sales or fictional payout.
6. **Execution logs are not admitted learning.** Continuous play explicitly grants
   no training admission. The existing native lesson recorder is teacher-selected;
   existing player dataset admission requires declared plans and authenticated
   streams. Reuse these contracts, but never relabel a forced action or old log as
   a comparative model choice. The continuous-to-training connection must be proved
   before promising that further play will train the goal policy.
7. **Independent evaluation supply is a scheduling dependency.** Previous origins
   are consumed. Different saves, seeds and copies are not independent ancestry.
   Check eligible source metadata early, without browsing held-out outcomes. An
   unresolved source gap permits honest TRAIN diagnostics, not a promotion claim.
