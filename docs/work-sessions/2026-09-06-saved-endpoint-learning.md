   teacher route or losing earlier training examples and unsuccessful outcomes.
2. **Learned authority:** the existing full-support sampler chooses among supported semantic
   goals; deterministic mechanics execute them. Fit only actually executed sampled outcomes.
3. **Transfer test:** short changed-state Red continuation after fitting; same lineage, not an
   independent evaluation or proof of ROM-modification/Crystal transfer.

5. **Time box:** one session, at most four hours. No full replay, new teacher factory or CI-only loop.
6. **Stop condition:** unsafe state, missing provenance, no settled sampled experience or failed
   retention check ends the declared attempt without retry. Do not increase limits for favorable data.

expansion. Preserve the original train lineage and old diagnostic archives. The established

contrast. Evolution of needed precursors and transferable living collection remain the objective.
