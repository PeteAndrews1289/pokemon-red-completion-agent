# Overnight outcome-trained damage proposal — rejected

The experiment fitted one new damage head from 96 TRAIN configurations and
compared it with frozen K on 48 withheld configurations. It improved aggregate
one-turn damage ranking, but failed the frozen coverage and family guards. The
candidate is **rejected**. No composed battle, story input, promotion, or
publication followed. [Path-free evidence](../evidence/red-damage-proposal-overnight-2026-09-23.json).

## Boundaries and counts

- Four authenticated, reused TRAIN origins; no new independent root or
  cross-title transfer claim. Thirty-two fit families and 16 withheld families
  have disjoint unordered actor/opponent species pairs. Timing siblings and
  three HP/status/accuracy-stage contexts remain within their family.
- Four legal supported damaging alternatives per configuration, three timing
  offsets each: 1,152 fit and 576 holdout native branches. All 1,728 actions
  were forced measurement alternatives, never learned battle decisions.
- One fit attempted and completed: 16 hidden units, 600 epochs, learning rate
  0.03, expected-regret objective, seed 230902, no warm start. Compact-to-125
  embedding and exact serialization were verified. The admitted status/move,
  control and switch components remained byte-identical.
- The damage domain excludes charge/recharge, multi-hit, trapping, OHKO,
  fixed damage, Counter, self-destruct, drain and recoil. Secondary status and
  priority were represented. Unsupported mechanics remain a general-replacement
  blocker.

## Withheld result and stop decision

The aggregate mean improved by 0.09210 absolute (54.49% relative). Choices
changed in 29/48 configurations; paired regrets improved in 22, worsened in
six, and tied in 20. The 2,000-resample family-cluster descriptive interval
for absolute improvement is −0.01626 to 0.18675, conditional on four reused
TRAIN origins. It is not an independent-root confidence claim.

The frozen gate failed: level-31 configurations regressed by 0.11477 and the
high visible-stat-ratio slice by 0.05613, both over the 0.03 slice limit.
Family 40 regressed by 0.52744, over the 0.15 family limit. In that family,
against ghost/poison, the candidate ranked Fire Blast over Earthquake even
though measured Earthquake damage was higher across the neutral, burn and
accuracy-stage contexts. Three timing samples still vary; the large repeated
gap cannot be attributed to one sampled miss. Five worst-regret cases were
traced from saved observations, with features and choices recomputed; no case
was replayed. The 72 planned paired team/offset comparisons (144 episodes)
were not dispatched.

## Audit and remaining gate

Saved-event recomputation verified all 144 targets, 1,728 episode logs and
1,728 distinct retained terminal hashes. There were zero missing terminals,
explicit branch failures, illegal actions, or candidate serialization errors.
The first ROM-free retention audit failed because historical forced/prompt
records have no move head. Its failure receipt remains; one isolated classifier
repair completed the audit without a new native run or fit.

The prior opened 16-case TRAIN damage diagnostic is comparable in foe-HP units:
mean regret changed from 0.06794 to 0.05847. Other historical retention
groups use mixed battle-utility targets or lack a move choice, so they cannot
satisfy the 0.02 foe-HP retention guard. General replacement and morning
integration remain blocked independently of the failed holdout gate.

Stage 328 and the protected collection state kept their starting hashes;
stage 329 is absent. Story progress, registrations, actor authority, and
public repository state did not change. The next bounded experiment, if
separately authorized after Astra's review, is an independently rooted study
of supported move-effect coverage and outcome variance. This packet permits
no additional fit or replay.
