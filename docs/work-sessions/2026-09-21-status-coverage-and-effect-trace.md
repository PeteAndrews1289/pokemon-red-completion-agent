# Mechanics coverage and selected-effect observation

## Prospective mission check

1. Capability: teach status/recovery usefulness from measured effects across saves,
   without tying immune/occupied conditions to one source root.
2. Authority: maintenance unblocking the named measured-effect policy experiment.
   No player fit or authority change in this packet. Trace observations are training
   labels only, never actor features, masks or teacher replacements.
3. Transfer test: prospective recipes cover each required condition on every fitting
   root and a separate reserved TRAIN-root/configuration cohort. This is not independent
   natural-party evaluation. Verify instrumentation against identical uninstrumented
   controller runs before trusting its labels.
4. Cheapest falsifier: missing mechanics-by-root support, unmatched effect-entry/exit,
   or instrumentation changing native actions, frames, outcomes or terminal state.
5. Time box: 90 minutes. Action-free recipe validation plus at most eight distinct
   TRAIN contrast cases, each once instrumented and once uninstrumented, one selected
   turn each. At most 16 native diagnostic episodes, no fit or broad collection.
6. Stop: unverified ROM/hook boundary, trace mismatch, invalid execution, unsupported
   condition or paired-state divergence. Preserve failures; no retry of consumed cases,
   no live promotion and no earned-save access except read-only hash verification.

## Design

New packet identities and seed; never modify/retry the prior effect probe or open
the 128 reserved battle comparisons. Explicit source assignments replace positional
modulo assignment in the new builder. Cover every family/contrast on three fitting
roots; reserve the fourth root with a separate recipe variant. Validate required
immunity/occupied/floor/healing support before fitting any future learner.

Trace the verified revision-zero native JumpMoveEffect dispatcher at entry and
return, with read-only snapshots of effect-relevant state. Pair by actor, move,
effect and subject; report unknown on mismatches. No endpoint-only inference of
healing, confusion renewal or Disable. Hidden durations remain training telemetry.
PyBoy hooks temporarily instrument ROM execution; no RAM/controller mutation by
the observer. Remove hooks before endpoint persistence and compare exact native
state with an uninstrumented arm. Training setup interventions remain disclosed.

Native contrasts: Recover injured/full; Rest injured+burned/full; Confuse Ray
clear/already-confused; Disable clear/already-disabled. Initial conditions are
teacher setup only. One selected status action, no battle-learning or win claim.
Expected cost: at most 16 turns, 80,000 total frames, 90 minutes overall.

## Pre-execution correction

The first setup was rejected by the existing trainer stat-consistency guard,
before factory RAM writes or gameplay. Its failure is retained in the v1 packet;
zero diagnostic turns or pairs were consumed. Custom actor/opponent stat overrides
were invalid for this factory and are removed, not enabled by relaxing the guard.
The revised v2 plan prospectively chooses naturally slower opponents with ordinary,
non-priority attacks and cartridge-derived stats, before any observed outcome.
New case identities and a new source freeze bind this pre-execution correction.
The same maximum16diagnostic turns/zero fits applies across both packets. Once
native actions begin, any instrumentation/state divergence ends qualification.

## Verified result

Source e857cfec; v2 packet closed. All eight paired checks passed, covering 16
single-turn episodes, 112 authenticated events and 18,812 frames. Each instrumented
arm matched the uninstrumented arm's selected action, visible observations, resources,
frame count and exact terminal save bytes. Hooks were removed before persistence.
No invalid actions, actor telemetry features, learned decisions or model fits.

| Native effect | Applied | No effect | What was verified |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Recover | 1 | 1 | Injured actor gained 21 HP inside the effect; full-HP actor gained none. |
| Rest | 1 | 1 | Injured burned actor gained 21 HP and changed burn to sleep; full HP unchanged. |
| Confuse Ray | 1 | 1 | Clear opponent became confused; already-confused opponent was not renewed. |
| Disable | 0 | 2 | Both invocations returned unchanged; no successful native Disable example yet. |

The clear-target Disable attempt produced no effect. Retain it; do not retry that
case to obtain a desired label. The instrumentation pass is not a claim that both
Disable outcomes are native-qualified. ROM-free tests cover the positive transition.
All diagnostic foes were the same prospectively selected species with native stats:
this tiny workload verifies observation, not broad matchup or move performance.

The new crossed builder plans 192 fitting recipes on three TRAIN roots and 32
reserved recipes on the fourth. Each root has all 32 family/contrast cells; immunity,
occupied major status, accuracy floor, healing and volatile-state conditions are
checked from the declared recipe, not guessed from its name. Explicit source IDs
are authenticated at materialization. None of that training/reserved collection ran.
The measured-support preflight rejects the previous predictor's fitting inventory
for absent immunity features; a complete plan never substitutes for observed labels.

Eight diagnostic effect labels were measured (three applied, five no-effect), but
they are qualification data, not an admitted training corpus. This is engineering
progress unblocking measured-effect learning, not a better learned battler. The
prior reward-corrected player remains rejected; no readiness gate was relaxed.
