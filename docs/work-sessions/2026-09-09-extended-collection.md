# Longer automatic collection training

## Working mission check

- Capability: sustain useful collection and legitimate supply/recovery over a longer automatic run, preserving living specimens and resource costs.
- Learned authority: actual native goal or regional destination choices, with eligible outcomes fitted and the next model loaded after each step. Deterministic mechanics remain disclosed.
- Transfer test: changed step/time budgets in ROM-free tests; live choices encounter changing inventory, party resources and search history. This remains one correlated Red lineage, not independent or cross-title evidence.
- Cheapest falsifier: continue native95's actual endpoint for at most16 steps, with a60-minute between-step deadline; stop early at the first unexpected failure. Measure captures, evolutions, new living species, supply costs and elapsed time.
- Time box: two hours for bounded integration, execution and closeout; expected gameplay/fit batch30–45 minutes if all16 steps remain feasible.
- Stop condition: no retries of consumed trials. Stop on unverifiable outcomes, unexpected execution failure, specimen loss, fitting failure, unsupported goal menus, the step limit or the elapsed-time boundary. Preserve an in-flight bounded episode rather than interrupt it just to meet a clock limit.

## Plan

1. Extend the existing runner's maximum declared step budget, add a monotonic between-step time boundary and explicit per-step progress. Keep historical defaults and existing guards.
2. Qualify the bounds and unchanged continuation semantics, then publish one executable change.
3. Run a newly declared continuation from native95 and its authentic Route1 endpoint. Do not edit the executable during the run. Inspect whether evolution options are genuinely available; do not count useful duplicate stock as completed living species.
4. Record results and remaining blockers in the dashboard, roadmap, handoffs and narratives. No new framework, full replay or sealed evaluation.
