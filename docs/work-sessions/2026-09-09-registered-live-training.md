- Learned authority: model-selected destinations and native collection/support goals produce
  actual registered-objective outcomes; deterministic execution is not learned combat.
- Transfer test: inherited registration and independent local stock in synthetic integration;
  the live batch is correlated Red training, not a transfer or held-out evaluation.

  new-objective corpus, reloadable fitted model, and exact terminal ledger agreement.

  ready, without starting a full replay or expanding the architecture.
- Stop condition: lost specimens, unverifiable checkpoint/ledger, objective mixing, unsafe
  control, or exhausted bounded attempt. Preserve failures and never replay consumed attempts.
