legitimate registrations to one shared Pokédex across games. Red is the current development game;
Blue, Crystal and a compatible unfamiliar Red modification remain later transfer tests.

the two four-productive-slot profiles over the two five-slot profiles and sampled candidate 1. This
is not a success claim. It exposes a concrete weakness for real outcome data to correct.

## Authority boundary

The model chose the Safari destination profile. Deterministic code still owns transport, admission,
movement, encounter handling and capture inputs. No gameplay occurred, no registration changed and
no training row was created. A later outcome becomes eligible only if the selected binding executes
once, the fresh ledger remains readable and the ordinary learning contract admits the observed arm.
