## Evidence boundary

- The failed outcome changed neither registered species nor collection credit.
- Teacher labels, action traces, independent evaluations and authority promotions are all zero.
- This is same-lineage in-sample training evidence. It does not establish fresh-game reliability,
  learned low-level control, unseen-ROM adaptation or cross-title transfer.

## Engineering finding

The second failure is not evidence that fishing itself is broken. It reveals a mismatch between
candidate eligibility and executor capability: a route was scored as executable even though it
required a scripted-dialogue interruption and the runner's declared dialogue budget was zero. The
correct next repair is a generic capability/eligibility boundary, not a special route to the missing
species and not a retry with a larger budget chosen after seeing the result.
