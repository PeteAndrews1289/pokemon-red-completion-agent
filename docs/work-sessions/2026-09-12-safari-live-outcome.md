# Model-selected Safari live outcome

## Reorientation

The product remains a hierarchical player that can finish Pokémon stories and add legitimate
registrations to one shared Pokédex across games. Red is the development environment. This session
tested one reusable acquisition family; it did not authorize Crystal work or a full-game replay.

Capability: turn an identity-free, model-selected Safari area into a retained missing registration
without a named-species route. Learned authority: model104 selected one of four real area profiles;
deterministic code retained transport, movement, menus and capture. Transfer test: the catalog and
patrol derive from cartridge encounter and terrain data. Cheapest falsifier: fail to turn the exact
selected area into one bounded readable outcome. Stop: one registration, resource exhaustion or the
first unrecoverable boundary.

## Result

The model-selected East-area attempt succeeded. One paid admission reached the selected area, a
terrain-derived two-tile patrol generated encounters and the observed Safari command controller
retained one previously missing registration. Collection moved from **79 to 80 registered**, from
**59 to 60 living species**, and from **63 to 64 specimens**. The final successful patrol observed
12 encounters, 11 flees and one capture, leaving 23 Safari Balls and 235 steps.

Across the retained continuation chain, the system executed 1,271 counted controller actions and
152,580 emulator frames. Every failed identity remained closed; each successor resumed its exact
state rather than replaying the admission or resampling the model choice.

## What the failures taught us

- The Safari step allowance is a two-byte counter; the first postcondition read only one byte.
- A random encounter during the patrol approach is a valid handoff, not failed navigation.
- Safari's battle menu has distinct observed left/right layouts and cannot use a blind command
  sequence.
- After a thrown Ball, settlement must observe the battle phase; a stale cursor can survive while a
  natural flee is still resolving.

These were integration failures at real emulator boundaries. The fixes are generic to Safari
execution and are covered by focused tests. The model did not choose those low-level repairs.

## Learning boundary

Model104 remains model104. The area choice was committed before input and produced a useful outcome,
but this capability harness retained exact state hashes and aggregate counters rather than the
standard per-action episode journal consumed by the incremental fitter. The result is therefore
measured development evidence, not yet a fitted row. Retroactively calling it a normal traced
episode would weaken the project's claim boundary.

The next engineering gate is a declared **development-measured outcome trust tier**: authenticate
the committed menu, replay the model sample, verify before/after registration and physical stock,
retain aggregate costs and label the missing trace explicitly. Only then may the existing fitter
decide whether to admit this row. If that adapter cannot validate the record without inventing
facts, preserve the capture as zero-row support and collect the next family through the normal
episode journal.
