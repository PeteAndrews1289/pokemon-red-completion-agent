### Next-session finding: resource features need attention before more supply training

A short source audit found that `red_living_dex_setup_candidate_features` always encodes
`resource_cost=0.0`; the bounded goal scorer reuses that projection. Its resource-pressure context
comes from capture/recovery item headroom, not spendable money. The current causal outcome adapter
also measures resource cost as the decrease in capture plus recovery items; shopping money is not
part of that target. The deterministic Mart provider
checks affordability, but that does not teach the scorer the difference between buying near its
reserve and spending most of its money. This is a representation limitation, not proof that it
alone caused the observed choice. A larger batch of identical supply successes would not repair it.

Before the next fitting batch, choose the smallest explicit treatment of known purchase costs and
reserve deficits: deterministic cost quotes/budget constraints can complement learned success and
progress predictions; prices need not be rediscovered by a model. If a new learned target is needed,
version its semantics instead of silently reinterpreting old consumable-only costs. Add only the
necessary prospective, identity-free cost/reserve contrast. Preserve the old projection and all 29 original
rows verbatim; do not reinterpret or fabricate their features/targets. Qualify changed-reserve
synthetic cases first, then collect a small declared train batch through actual selected actions.
Time-box this to one session and prefer the existing learner/provider interfaces over a new
orchestration layer. Controller-capable terminal continuation remains a separate next seam.
