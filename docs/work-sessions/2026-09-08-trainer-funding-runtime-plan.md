   money and defeated-event progress, and reconnect legitimate supply/collection.

move_slot_policy, timing, maximum_intro_pulses=32, maximum_settle_pulses=32)`

`TrainerFundingCandidate`. `validate_target()` raises on a stale cartridge/live
