# Retained-curriculum replay: prospective plan

## Mission check

1. Capability: improve late-battle reserve selection without forgetting earlier
   measured battle decisions. No new runtime feature or scripted action rule.
2. Learned authority: fit existing switch/control heads only. Damage, status,
   feature schemas, inference and deployed authority stay unchanged.
3. Transfer: only a passing fit can open the retained 24 unplayed constructed
   comparison teams, three timings and three actors (216 episodes). These are
   correlated TRAIN-root compositions, not independent natural-game evaluation.
4. Cheapest falsifier: ROM-free tests prove a non-anchor retention lesson affects
   the update, duplicates cannot overweight it, and hard budgets remain binding.
5. Time box: one session, at most two hours; one offline two-stage fitting attempt.
6. Stop: malformed bindings, failed tests, failed fit gate, censored comparison,
   or failed comparison gate. No retry, parameter sweep, promotion or campaign play.
