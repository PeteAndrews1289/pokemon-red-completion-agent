## Durable campaign implementation

The launcher now requires a canonical private plan, exact committed source bundle, clean published HEAD, exact ROM and source-state digests, and one successful exact-source CI run. A new campaign epoch writes its own durable assignment and claim before any case input. It checkpoints case opening/closure and shared budget snapshots, then seals complete or failed. A completed, failed or partial epoch cannot restart or resume.

## Frozen campaign and result

Campaign B froze four new identities: three Route 11 controls spanning different party menus/timing and one Diglett's Cave cross-venue case. Case limits were 2,000 attempted actions and 200,000 actual frames; campaign limits were 12,000 actions and 2,000,000 frames. Forced switching, move learning and same-battler move replacement remained explicit coverage gaps.

The first Route 11 control stopped after **893 attempted / 893 completed actions** and **80,081 actual frames**. Its terminal evidence reopened with:

- phase `battle`, reason `battle_failed`, runtime phase `policy_selection`;
- healthy journal, known cost and no action in flight;
- 75 retained move selections;
- active party index 3 at 44 HP, opponent at 34 HP;
- all four active move PP values exhausted;
- no retry permission.

The final five retained selections show opponent HP staying at 34 while the last available PP was spent. The next MAIN-menu policy decision had no usable move. This establishes a generic missing contingency: the maintenance battle controller cannot recover when the active battler cannot make progress and exhausts its usable PP. It does **not** establish the opponent's exact identity or a specific type-immunity cause.

The campaign failed and sealed itself. All three later episode directories are absent, so the later Route 11 controls and Diglett's Cave case did not open. Campaign B and its first case are consumed permanently. There is no patch or replay in this session.
