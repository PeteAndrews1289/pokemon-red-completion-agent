## What changed in the system

The checkpoint lineage now permits a registered measured terminal to follow another registered
measured terminal only after recursively authenticating the prior derived chain. Generic
support-on-support lineage remains forbidden.

The traversal observer now types visible, non-battle, input-blocking text as scripted dialogue.
The route interruption handler can resolve it only when given an explicit bounded pulse budget. It
uses CANCEL plus stabilization, rejects battle transitions and non-ready terminals, and reports the
actual resumed position so routing replans rather than assuming the player stayed put.

These are reusable mechanics. No map, coordinate or species identity entered the policy features,
and no failed choice was retried or relabeled.

## Claim boundary

This is bounded same-lineage development, not independent evaluation. The measured episodes have
authenticated aggregate costs and exact state transitions but no per-action journal, so both new
rows are training-only and ineligible for authority promotion. The fact that the next sampled
candidate changed after fitting the failure is evidence that the online loop adapts; it does not by
itself prove causal policy improvement or efficient fishing across seeds.

Fresh-game autonomy, complete Red registration, an unfamiliar Red modification, Blue/shared-ledger
continuity and Crystal transfer remain unfinished. Deterministic code still executes movement,
menus, battles, fishing and capture beneath the model's high-level destination choice.

## Reorientation

- **Reusable capability:** retain a failed strategic choice, recover without learning credit, fit,
  choose again and continue from the exact earned state.
- **Learned authority:** the fitted model controls the fishing destination ranking and sample.
- **Transfer test:** unchanged; the immediate prerequisite is diversity across Red destinations and
  acquisition families before freezing a policy for an unfamiliar Red modification.
- **Cheapest falsifier:** model108 cannot expose another useful multi-option menu, or its selected
  goal cannot settle under the current bounded executor contract.
- **Time box:** one action-free menu inspection plus one bounded selected outcome.
- **Stop condition:** forced-only support, identity-bearing policy features, a named-species route,
  hidden retry or an unverifiable terminal.

for the broadest honest acquisition menu. Prefer a useful cross-family choice. If only fishing has
