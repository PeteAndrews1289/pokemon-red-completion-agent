Status: bounded engineering complete; live integration remains. This is engineering toward the next funded collection lesson,
not a new learned result. AM01/model65 remain the current played endpoint.

## Mission check

1. **Capability:** recognize a useful supply shortfall and measure legitimate income,
   spending and consumable losses without confusing a quote with earned money.
2. **Learned authority:** prepare explicit earn-versus-purchase features and measured
   economy targets. Current funding is forced/deterministic support; no claim that
   model65 chooses when or how to earn. Do not silently relabel its old outcomes.
3. **Transfer test:** the same semantic representation must distinguish poor/rich
   states and profitable/unprofitable outcomes without species or map identities.
4. **Cheapest falsifier:** equal-cost offers with different income must differ in
   features; buying balls must not hide a consumed healing item; missing cash must
   remain unknown. Inspect the exact AM save without controller input.
5. **Time box:** one bounded implementation session for the observation/target gap
   and funding feasibility, then reassess before any League or Pay Day execution.
6. **Stop condition:** tests and saved-state inspection identify the smallest safe
   next income trial, or show an unmet prerequisite. No speculative full replay,
   unqualified League solo, save edits, forced-choice fit, or historical relabeling.
