# Model111 route-capability gate

## Outcome

The resource router now fails closed before ranking unless its configured interruption handler
explicitly declares support for every dynamic route boundary the generic Red transport may meet:
wild encounters, trainer engagement, active trainer battles and scripted dialogue. The declaration
is derived from the handler's actual finite budgets and survives both guarded collection recovery
and registered travel-capture wrappers.

The generic resource route now allows at most 128 wild exits, 8 trainer battles and 4 scripted
dialogues. Those allowances share the route's total140-interruption bound. A handler missing even
one required capability leaves the goal unavailable instead of placing an unsafe destination in
the model's menu.

This closes the engineering defect revealed by model110's retained failure. It does not replay that
choice, recover the game, add a label or fit a model. Model111 and the exact failure checkpoint are
unchanged at 83 registrations, 63 living species, 67 specimens and 111 settled training-only rows.
