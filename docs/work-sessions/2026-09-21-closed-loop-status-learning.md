## Prospective mission check

1. Capability: status/recovery timing without repetition, with native consequences.
2. Learned authority: learned status versus frozen K's best legal damage move;
   no repeated-move ban, status-rule teacher or live-policy substitution.
3. Transfer: a fresh generated configuration cohort, frozen before collection and
   unopened until TRAIN iteration finishes. Related TRAIN ancestry is not natural
   independent transfer; no automatic live promotion or campaign action.
4. Cheapest falsifier: TRAIN continuations still reward repeated ineffective status
   or the fitted actor repeats it despite strongly worse measured returns.
5. Time box: two hours, with at most three prospectively bounded TRAIN policy
   iterations and one withheld comparison for this packet. User asks to continue
   to a working state; reassess at the boundary rather than silently extending it.
6. Stop: retain every failure. No replay/refit on any consumed withheld cohort,
   hidden information, campaign edits, gate weakening or publication. If iteration
   fails, close this design and identify the actual cause before a distinct packet.
