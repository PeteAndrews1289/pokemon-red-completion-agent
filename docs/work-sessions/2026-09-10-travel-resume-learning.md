## What the model chose, and what it did not

The stock-based deterministic inventory proposed the available Koffing evolution objective.
The learned policy chose whether to pursue it against other genuine goals. The policy uses
25% uniform exploration mixed with75% model softmax; success is not evidence that every
selected choice was greedy or optimal. Navigation, battle, capture and evolution mechanics
remain deterministic skills beneath these learned choices.

MtMoon1F, MansionB1F and MtMoonB1F were prospective collection-source proposals for the three
menus. None became the executed acquisition goal. They are not three successful journeys,
three additional destination training examples or additional collection gains. All three
admitted labels are actual native-goal choices; there were no forced singleton labels.

This is the intended registration-first pattern in a bounded setting: retain a useful catch,
evolve it only as far as needed, keep historical Pokédex credit and continue useful resource
work. It does not establish an autonomous player from game startup, complete collection,
independent improvement or Blue/Crystal transfer. No arbitrary level target was introduced.
