## Trust boundary

This is a useful training example, but it is lower-trust than a normal replay-verified episode. The
capability harness did not retain its standard per-action journal. The adapter therefore records
that limitation as data: training-only, no action trace, no independent evaluation and no authority
promotion. It cannot silently become sealed evidence later.

## Engineering review
