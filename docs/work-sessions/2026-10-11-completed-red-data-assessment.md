## The strategic selection findings

The deployed broad goal model contains 151 settled examples, 356 input dimensions
and 60 dimensions with nonzero learned coefficients. The retained earlier audit of
this unchanged corpus found rank 37 in its 61-column weighted design. Having many
possible observation fields is not the same as informative training coverage.

Direct parameter inspection confirms that all nine recent-loss interactions and
all nine engaged-opponent-level-gap interactions have zero means and zero
coefficients in this model. Its preparation HP and PP coordinates also have zero
coefficients. Other level, resource and readiness channels can still influence
choices; a separate scoped readiness head is not identical to this broad model.
But we cannot say this broad learner has learned to respond to those absent
channels merely because they appear in a log.

The 908 standard-head explanation menus contain 8,534 candidate rows. Of those,
8,013 have raw success predictions above one and therefore clip to one—**93.9%**.
This is regression saturation, not a 93.9% probability of success. These explanation
menus cover the broad head; 174 other model-owned receipts use scoped selection
identities/formats and are not silently treated as missing broad-head explanations.

Across all 1,082 model-owned receipts, 661 use exploration and 421 use greedy
selection. Among the 908 explained broad-head menus, 378 selected indices differ
from the argmax. That is consistent with the declared exploration policy, not by
itself an execution mistake. Neither the chosen sampling mass nor the score margin
is calibrated confidence.

In the main segment family, the median selected candidate's largest standardized
coordinate is approximately 21, and the maximum is approximately 98. These are
warning signs of extrapolation from narrow TRAIN coverage, not statistical
uncertainty estimates. Earlier PP analysis of this same model found acquisition
states at 0.857–1.0 while the relevant TRAIN band ended near 0.138; this was not a
unit-conversion error.

The operational lesson is to teach useful contrasts through the existing TRAIN
bridge: loss versus success, healthy versus depleted important combat members,
and enough preparation versus additional unnecessary grinding. Do not repeatedly
refit essentially the same narrow failures, fit this DEVELOPMENT run, blindly
clamp inputs or insert species/source IDs as shortcuts.

### The fresh entry mismatch

The inspected [fresh-start entry](../../scripts/run_fresh_red_player.py) calls the
native player with greedy_selection=True. The successful continuation entry can
declare exploration and passes validated readiness refinement and related options
to the native loop. The fresh entry does not forward the same refinement arguments.

The fresh entry also accumulates cash from before/after goal net differences.
The [continuation entry](../../scripts/run_configured_red_player.py) already prefers
the native observed-debit meter, which preserves purchases and losses inside a
net-profitable goal. The fresh loop stops at any typed outcome error; known normal
loss/settlement and safe partial-dose renewal must be distinguished from unknown
execution faults without swallowing the latter.

These are specific source findings, not speculation about a new architecture.
A package pin and a short startup smoke do not prove fresh/continuation feature
parity. Do not refreeze the stale launch path and assume the complete earned
continuation guarantees it will work.

Menu/boundary work is **40.9% of execution plus menu time**. It is not 40.9% of
the whole calendar session. The cumulative native-accounted ledger totals
57,828.56 seconds, about 16.06 hours. The original launch to collection closeout
spans about 31.3 calendar hours; engineering, training, inspections, waiting and
coordination account for time outside those native receipt phases.

Local battle-policy time across the completed journals totals about 51.67 seconds.
The final collection block alone spent 5.37 hours executing skills and 2.11 hours
building/observing menus. Faster assistant responses or emulator rendering cannot
remove repeated route feasibility, quotation, recovery and XP work.

Evolution accounts for about 4.41 hours of main-family skill execution. The two
Dragonair partials alone used about an hour, but retained XP; that hour was not
automatically wasted. The correct improvement is resumable useful doses and better
stopping, not discarding progress or declaring the partial target completed.

A hypothetical 50% reduction in menu time would save about 3.24 native-accounted
hours; it would not halve the entire project. Existing per-inventory route caching
and cheap exclusions are the right starting points. Reuse immutable topology and
pure quotes across equivalent observations only with correct invalidation for
position, access, stock, party, defeated trainers and other relevant state.
Preserve exactly the same legal offers and costs.
