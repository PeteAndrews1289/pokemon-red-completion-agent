## Implemented and verified

The tracked `qualify_repeatable_red_wild_battle` entry wraps materializer and shared battle runtime
in one durable episode. Assignment and claim precede source restoration. The materializer checks
the authenticated source inside that episode, announces its phase and uses a supplied metered
executor for every setup path, including direct policy-boundary actions outside its older counter.
The fixed mechanics policy remains explicitly maintenance-only.

The journal separates admitted attempts from completed macros and actual emulator frames.
Case and campaign action limits refuse before dispatch; frame limits refuse before a tick.
Costs accumulate across sessions without summing restored frame origins. Partial ticks retain their
actual measured delta and original exception. An unreadable counter explicitly marks cost unknown.
Silent short ticks or overruns fail with their observed cost, not an invented nominal value.

Every phase, action boundary and tick admission/reconciliation is synchronized. Known materializer
errors become bounded semantic reason codes; arbitrary error text is not stored. Shared battle
diagnostics live inside the same outer failure record. Successful and failed episodes must reopen
exactly. Any exception, storage fault or readback failure closes the campaign. Published episodes
are not aborted again after a readback error; completed executors cannot send further input.

The broader tests caught two defects during implementation: primitive calls initially lived outside
the permitted executor boundary, and real battle diagnostics contained tuples that compare
unequally with reopened JSON lists. Both were fixed and regression-tested without weakening gates.
