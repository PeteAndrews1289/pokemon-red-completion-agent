## Implementation contract

The frozen policy identifier is
`fixed_strongest_usable_move_with_stall_switch_v1`. Existing plans retain
`fixed_strongest_usable_move`; a different policy changes the plan digest.

The intervention runs at a confirmed living wild-battle MAIN boundary inside the original shared
runtime, before move selection. It observes exact active move/PP state, counts only selected-slot
PP spends, resets on enemy HP decrease and rejects unexpected move/battler/encounter transitions.
No-spend turns do not advance the stall count. Species/map identities are not policy exceptions;
species feeds the existing adapter matchup projection and identity checks only reject state changes.

Existing reserve ranking screens for healthy status, half HP, level within five below the opponent,
and an ordinary non-immune damaging move with PP. It does not promise survival on entry.
The controller durably claims one switch before input, rechecks state/menu, uses the same metered
executor, and verifies target identity, positive HP, unchanged encounter/bag and MAIN settlement.
It never restarts the battle loop or refunds the switch. A second stall is a retained failure.

This threshold is a resource heuristic, not proof of immunity, misses or a stuck battle.
The domain is a disposable WIN qualification, not capture, status strategy or full-player control.
Fainted forced switching, move learning, same-battler move replacement and cross-venue cartridge
settlement remain unqualified. Prior roadmap wording conflating the two switch boundaries is corrected.
