# Model112 automatic-fishing failure learning

## Outcome

The automatic Red runtime derived four reachable productive fishing destinations from the
cartridge and joined them with one ordinary restore option. The resulting five-choice menu was
created without controller input, exposed two goal kinds and carried no map, species or coordinate
identity into the model features. Model111 selected an acquisition through exploration. Cross-box
capture support attached to that already selected binding without asking the policy again.

The selected run did not complete. After 228 controller actions and 16,668 emulator frames, its
route reached a scripted dialogue while the live executor's dialogue allowance was still zero.
The exact attempt is consumed and was not retried. Registrations remained 83 and all 67 specimens
were retained.

That failure is now useful data. Its frozen menu, propensity, selected arm, source, bounds, claim,
terminal and economy evidence were reconstructed without input and admitted as one training-only
observed outcome. Model111 became model112 with 112 settled examples; all 111 earlier examples
remain in the corpus. The model still has bounded-development authority only.

## Reusable repair

The production route executor now allows at most four generic scripted-dialogue interruptions.
This repair applies only to future choices. It does not change, replay or relabel the consumed
model111 outcome. The measured-choice validator also recognizes the exact historical declaration
shape and derives its goal kind from the already frozen menu and selected index rather than adding
a field after gameplay.

Tampering with the frozen frame/cast limits, CI identity, selected binding, retry status or teacher
count fails closed. Legacy mixed-menu declarations retain their original stricter shape.
