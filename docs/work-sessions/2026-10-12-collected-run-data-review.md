## Findings that stand

The existing infrastructure has completed the declared 124 registrations under
assisted development; it is worth retaining. The deployed goal head and seed are

Candidate multiplicity really does alter the sampler's family probabilities.
At the first slow evolution, nine evolution candidates receive 62.9% combined
probability against 21.3% for the higher-scoring story candidate. Existing greedy
selection removes that sampling effect without changing weights. The inspected

## Corrections to the interpretation

**A high story score is not evidence of learned readiness.** Re-reading all 293
diagnostics yields 315 available story/access candidates. Every one has raw
success above one, clipped to one, and negative action/frame costs clipped to
zero. Raw success ranges from 3.129 to 8.086. These are regression outputs, not
probabilities. Saturation alone does not prove a bad choice, but it removes the
basis for interpreting the score as reliable confidence about a battle.

In addition to the already identified zero challenge and preparation blocks,
all 28 story-specific readiness coefficient rows and all 28 development-specific
readiness rows are zero. Shared features can still affect both scores. This is
a narrower and better-supported claim than saying the entire player ignores
health or never learns preparation: recovery, capture and evolution have fitted
readiness terms, and the battlers are separate models.

**The economic prediction also drives the ordering.** Holding recorded menus and
outputs fixed, removing only the economic score adjustment would change the
best goal family in 164 of 293 menus. In 136 cases the base utility's restore
winner becomes story after that adjustment. This is an algebraic diagnostic,
not evidence that removing the economic head would improve play. At the first
evolution, the economic adjustment is +0.385 for story and -1.000 for evolution;
both include out-of-range predictions clipped before scoring. A selective
diagnosis must examine both value prediction and sampling.

**Most story offers are navigation.** Of the 315 story/access candidates, 279
are transport bindings, 27 are story battle bindings and nine are other story
actions. “Story highest in 289 menus” is not 289 independent decisions to face
a boss. Twenty goals can therefore be twenty small movements. Use real game
milestones for acceptance; make 20 decisions a review checkpoint, not the main
success criterion or an automatic end to useful movement.

**The slow work is not all wasted work.** Evolution earns eight registrations
and retains XP in five incomplete goals. Much of that evolution is eventually
required for 124. Delaying it may improve early progression and access to better
training, but 56 minutes of evolution is not a proven 56-minute end-to-end saving.
Nor would removing exploration preserve the observed training trajectory: all
24 selected development goals ranked below story in these menus. Twenty-three
occurred before Brock and one before Misty. Greedy replay would take a different
path; whether the skipped preparation was useful requires native evidence.

**Execution failures still determine whether a run finishes.** The 17 nonordinary
failed boundaries in the recent audit are not explained away by sampling. The
current r29 has no unexpected error and stopped at Pete's request, but a selector
change does not repair unrelated navigation, dialogue or battle-control defects.
The original report does not justify replacing the battler or promising that
greedy mode will finish Red.

**An old readiness component is not automatically a working connection.** The
October 6 League ownership audit's two valid prepared arms record zero actual
child-head queries; the earlier arms with the preview defect remain invalid.
This does not invalidate every later readiness result. It does show why the next
session must verify actual policy activation and scope, rather than treating a
head file or historical successful battle as proof of integration.

Original audit evidence, run receipts and retained state are unchanged.
