# Funded model104 collection continuation

Red returned from the resource detour to actual collection learning. A guarded support bridge
restored a practical capture reserve, the retained player caught Psyduck at Seafoam, and the next
three-option menu let the learned goal policy choose and complete Psyduck's evolution into Golduck.
The new terminal has **77 registered species**, **58 living species**, **62 specimens** and **47
required Red registrations remaining**. The registered-objective learner advanced from 103 to 104
settled examples.

## What actually ran

The earlier League attempts had already earned enough money but did not complete the rematch. A
controlled blackout retained half the carried funds and healed the party, after which deterministic
shop support bought 19 capture balls. Those support segments total 2,556 actions and 232,774 frames.
Their import into the registered player advanced zero frames, sent zero inputs and created zero
training examples.

The support bridge is intentionally described more narrowly than a native replay: each retained
segment has authenticated declaration, claim, result, source and state-chain hashes, but no retained
action trace. Public code admits this only through a distinct registered-support checkpoint type;
it cannot be relabeled as learned or native traced play.

The automatic continuation then completed two gameplay steps:

- Seafoam was the sole ordinary regional source exposed by the current enumerator. The forced
  singleton used Surf, encountered 14 wild Pokémon, fled 13 times and captured Psyduck. It used
  1,112 actions and 67,704 frames, raising registrations from 75 to 76. It was not fitted.
- The next native menu contained three goals. Model103 selected `evolve_species`; deterministic
  mechanics trained Psyduck into Golduck in 16,628 actions and 1,538,894 frames. Registrations rose
  from 76 to 77, and that actual selected outcome fitted model104.

The loop then stopped cleanly with `no_executable_native_goal`. It did not crash, replay a consumed
episode or substitute an undocumented teacher decision.

## What the stop teaches us

Funding is no longer the immediate blocker. The current candidate layer knows ordinary grass
sources and already-owned level evolutions. Both are exhausted at this save, while the cartridge
catalog still lists 47 legitimate missing registrations across evolution, water/wild encounters,
fishing, Safari, static encounters, trades, prizes, fossils and gifts.

The next milestone is therefore acquisition breadth, not another League attempt and not Crystal.
The planner should convert acquisition kinds from the cartridge-derived catalog into executable,
resource-aware candidates. The first implementation must be reusable by method and prerequisite;
it must not become 47 hand-written species routes.

## Reorientation

- **North star:** unchanged—learned high-level Pokémon play that completes stories and contributes
  to one shared registered Pokédex across games.
- **Measured learning:** one new model-selected successful outcome; model103 → model104.
- **Deterministic support:** funding, navigation, capture, battle and evolution execution remain
  skills beneath the learned selector.
- **Current falsifier:** after adding one acquisition family, the exact model104 save still exposes
  no useful executable goal, or making it executable requires a species-specific route exception.
- **Next session:** inventory the physical prerequisites at the model104 checkpoint, implement one
  generic acquisition-family provider, verify an action-free multi-option menu, then run one bounded
  model lesson only if the provider is genuinely generic.
- **Stop conditions:** no Crystal or Blue, no full replay, no forced outcome called learning, no
  route-coordinate policy feature, and no CI-only repair loop.

Recommended implementation model: **Sol High at standard speed**. Use Astra High or Max only for a
later architecture or authority-promotion decision.
