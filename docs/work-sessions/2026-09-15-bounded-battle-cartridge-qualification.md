## Adjudication

The cartridge qualification does **not** pass. The cheapest falsifier fired: a failed case was
durable but not sufficiently diagnostic or cost-complete. This is a runner evidence-contract gap,
not evidence that the repaired PP-vector invariant is wrong. The three successful cases remain
useful maintenance evidence but cannot be promoted into a partial pass.

The next design must durably checkpoint the component phase and cumulative costs around source
inspection, relocation, encounter materialization, battle execution and terminal settlement. Both
materializer and battle-runtime exceptions need a bounded sanitized semantic reason. A new runner
must prove those records with synthetic failures at multiple phases before a new cartridge identity
opens. The consumed Diglett case may not be retried or replaced inside this campaign.
