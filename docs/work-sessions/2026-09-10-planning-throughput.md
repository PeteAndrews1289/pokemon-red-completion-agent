# Planning throughput and fresh model7 continuation

## Mission check

1. Capability: practical repeated planning for missing-registration destinations.
2. Learned authority: unchanged by maintenance; unblock fresh model7 choices and
   fit only their actual played outcomes.
3. Transfer test: ROM-free maps with different warp layouts, observed starts,
   borders and graph mutations between calls; preserve route semantics exactly.
4. Cheapest falsifier: compare the optimized projection against the previous
   semantics across synthetic grids; unrelated exits must remain untouched.
5. Time box: two hours including a bounded fresh continuation and closeout.
6. Stop: changed safety semantics, unrecoverable input boundary, stale evidence,
   or insufficient time for another bounded attempt. Never replay consumed work.

unrelated warps absorbing and restrict departure from an observed warp start.
Use a fresh shallow mapping with replacements only at affected coordinates;
immutable edge tuples can be shared. No global cache or cross-state reuse.
Flash High receives a narrow read-only correctness review; Codex owns the patch,
measurement, controller execution and publication.

Current distance: seven registered-objective examples toward the next twelve-row
review threshold,40 verified registrations, and no full-Pokedex or cross-title
competence claim. The completed three-item integration checklist is not Phase5
completion; the remaining work is useful throughput, broader acquisition and
evolution coverage, sustained collection and measured generalization.
