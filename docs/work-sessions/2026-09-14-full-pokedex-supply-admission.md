# Full-Pokédex supply admission rejects a fourth blind source

## Decision

Do not select or claim source four. The present direct-origin architecture cannot guarantee that
an unopened catalog state exposes both capture and evolution before its root is irreversibly
claimed. Selection deliberately uses metadata rather than private menu quality; the two-family
proposal runs only after the claim and payload open. A failed gate therefore consumes a source
before any model query, choice, outcome or fit. Three sources already ended without a lesson.

The strict two-family gate is correct and remains unchanged. The rejected alternative is to inspect
several protected payloads before claim and choose the first passing menu. Although both external
reviews proposed that ordering, it would turn the nine remaining roots into a searchable private
menu bank, bypass the claim-first anti-cherry-picking boundary and make their apparent availability
depend on unclaimed payload access.

The smallest legitimate supply change is a prospectively declared **correlated training reset**
from the already authenticated Model121 terminal. It is one known train-lineage checkpoint, not a
new independent root, held-out history or fresh-run result. A zero-action gate over that reusable
checkpoint can fail without spending a fourth source. If it passes, one write-ahead model choice
may execute once and retain its success, failure or interruption, resource costs and fresh local
registration observation.

## Why the existing code cannot do that yet

The Model121 terminal is authenticated, train-partitioned and has86 local registrations, but its
checkpoint explicitly records `training_eligible: false` and has zero matches in the fixed context
catalog. Direct full-local training requires a catalog-authenticated root-pair claim. Ordinary
continued training supports correlated reuse only when the original capture is a unique exclusive
train catalog member. Admitting this terminal therefore needs a new versioned registered-reset
schema; silently weakening the catalog check would be provenance laundering.

The new schema must bind the exact parent episode/checkpoint/state, registered objective, Model121,
one reset identity and the capture/evolution menu before sampling. It must say
`independent_evaluation: false`, create no new upstream-lineage claim, forbid fallback/redraw/retry,
execute only the selected binding, freshly observe the terminal ledger and costs after ordinary
failure as well as success, and fit only a settled measured choice.
