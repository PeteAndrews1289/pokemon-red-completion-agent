# Fossil acquisition and owned-evolution continuation

Red added two registrations without weakening the learning boundary. The generic fossil provider
revived Omanyte, and the existing cartridge-derived evolution planner then found and completed
Omanyte's evolution into Omastar. The retained endpoint now contains **79 registered species, 59
living species and 63 specimens**, with **45 required Red registrations remaining**.

[Machine-readable evidence](../evidence/red-fossil-owned-evolution-2026-09-12.json).

## What changed

The fossil executor had reached the Cinnabar revival transaction but inspected storage before the
game finished its nickname flow. Cartridge source shows that the box species list is updated before
the full boxed-mon structure is written; the apparent mismatch was a legitimate in-progress
transaction. The executor now declines the nickname only after the Pokédex bit appears, then waits
for dialogue and storage to settle before accepting the result.

The interrupted attempt was not replayed. Two bounded, authenticated recovery steps completed the
already-started transaction. The final recovery used 8 actions and 816 frames, preserved every
prior registration and specimen, and added exactly one Omanyte registration and specimen.

From that durable save, an action-free inventory found exactly one executable native goal:
cartridge evolution data identified owned Omanyte as the precursor for missing Omastar. One bounded
forced episode completed ten level gains in 27,215 actions and 2,293,481 frames. It retained 63
specimens and changed registrations from 78 to 79. Living-species count stayed at 59 because the
same specimen evolved.

## What did not happen

Neither goal involved a genuine choice among alternatives. Both remain deterministic support and
created **zero new settled model examples**. Model104 and its 104-example corpus are unchanged. This
is meaningful collection and capability progress, but not evidence that the model learned a new
decision or can autonomously finish Red.

## Reorientation

The current grass/water regional enumerator and owned level-evolution inventory now expose no goal.
The 45 missing registrations break down into 13 evolutions, 8 ordinary wild/water encounters, 7
fishing, 6 Safari, 4 static, 3 in-game trades, 2 prizes, 1 fossil and 1 gift.

The next reusable capability is generic item evolution. Several missing targets have already-owned
precursors, so it can create useful work without a species-specific route. The session must first
read actual stones, party/box contents and shop access without input. It may then expose candidates
from cartridge evolution requirements and real inventory or procurement. A successful singleton is
still support; model training resumes only when the live menu contains genuine alternatives.

- **North Star:** unchanged—model-led Pokémon play and one shared, verified Pokédex across games.
- **Cheapest falsifier:** the exact save has no executable item-evolution candidate, or execution
  requires a species-specific route or policy feature.
- **Stop conditions:** no Blue or Crystal, no full replay, no consumed-state retry, no forced result
  called learning, and no repeated CI-only repair loop.
- **Next implementation model:** Sol High with Fast enabled. Reserve Astra High/Max for a later
  architecture or authority-promotion audit.
