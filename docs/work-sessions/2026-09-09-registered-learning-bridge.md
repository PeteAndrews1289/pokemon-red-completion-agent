# Registered observation, checkpoint and learning bridge

## Mission check

- Capability: carry verified global registrations through real goal observations,
  durable continuations and outcome reconstruction, independently of living stock.
- Learned authority: maintenance unblocking `red-shared-registration-learning-v1`;
  the existing scorer will choose useful acquisition/evolution opportunities.
- Transfer test: inherited Blue credit, overlapping local flags, sole-precursor
  evolution, duplicate captures and physical reserves in ROM-free scenarios.
- Cheapest falsifier: a legitimate last-copy evolution must preserve credit;
  a duplicate must earn no novelty and a missing physical specimen must fail.
- Time box: one session, at most two hours; prioritize the existing runtime seam.
- Stop condition: no gameplay until observation, continuation and reward admission
  agree on the explicit objective. Never rewrite the historical 114 examples.

## Compatibility decision

Keep the historical model/corpus immutable. Registered-only outcomes use a new
objective identity and must not be silently pooled with living-Dex targets.
Historical parameters may later be evaluated as an explicitly disclosed warm
start; preserving weights is not evidence that the new objective has been trained.
