# Red battle clustered integration curriculum

Status: the first action-free information gate failed closed; no new battle outcome or model fit
has occurred.

## Why the seven-context design ended

The two-root relocation successor ran once from exact main and both assignments ended at the same
ready Red state after 111 executed route steps. The route planner reported `planner_no_route` at
map 19, coordinate `(27, 7)`. Both roots are permanently consumed. This satisfies the declared
stop condition: do not harden a selected Red route merely to fill an arbitrary denominator.

The historical V2 experiment remains immutable. It still required one retained plus seven fresh
train contexts and eight development contexts. It is retired incomplete, not edited to claim that
five equals seven.

## Mission check

1. **Reusable capability:** learn a title-neutral battle preference from all cartridge-measured
   legal actions at short authenticated battle states.
2. **Learned authority:** a successful cycle produces one non-authoritative candidate that can
   later replace the fixed move preference inside a separately bounded shadow/authority gate.
3. **Transfer test:** after a separate untouched Red gate, the identical supported feature/action
   projection is compared in Crystal as Red-initialized versus zero-initialized learning.
4. **Cheapest falsifier:** authenticate the five retained fresh captures and reject the roster
   action-free if its support, diversity, claim availability, independence, hidden-contrast rank,
   or numerical clearance is inadequate.
5. **Time box:** one engineering session to publish the catalog/admission seam; one later
   action-free qualification before any outcome collection.
6. **Stop condition:** no replacement, clone, outcome-selected row, route patch, or fit is allowed
   after a failed information gate. The roster closes without opening development.

## Prospective design

The successor uses exactly:

- one already measured retained train context, reopened without controller input;
- all five authentic fresh predecessor captures, with no selection after outcomes;
- all eight separately authenticated development captures, held disjoint from train.

For each fresh train capture, the executor restores the exact starting state separately for every
supported usable move and records the cartridge outcome. The learner receives one preference
example per upstream capture. An example with four legal moves does not outweigh one with three:
the loss is averaged across the six train contexts, and the target within each context is uniform
over its best measured action or actions.

The action-free information gate requires:

- six unique train and eight unique development lineages, states, observations, roots, and menu
  clusters;
- all five fresh train and at least six development contexts to expose three or more actions;
- at least two venues, two prior-margin strata, and two party conditions on both partitions;
- the full attainable hidden-contrast rank up to rank 14, plus singular-value clearance;
- exact current claim availability for every fresh root;
- no outcome, preferred action, teacher choice, or identity-bearing policy feature.

Only a passing gate opens one complete train collection and one train-only descriptive fit. The
base model, updated model, and fixed heuristic must commit their development choices before any
development outcome is opened. The eight same-state comparisons are descriptive and paired; they
cannot grant authority or support an inferential performance claim.

## What this does not claim

This is the first small battle update, not a general Pokémon player. Navigation, party development,
inventory, multi-step collection planning, living-Pokédex execution, sealed Red evaluation, and
Crystal transfer remain separate unfinished capabilities. The value of this gate is that it starts
real outcome learning without spending more days manufacturing Red-specific teacher routes.
