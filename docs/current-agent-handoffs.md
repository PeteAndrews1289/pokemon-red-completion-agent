## Phase mission checks

### Gates 0–2: source and prior

- Capability: trustworthy reuse of measured venue behavior across source revisions.
- Learned authority: none; bounded maintenance for the party experiment.
- Transfer test: the same compatibility/evidence contract must accept another title's adapter
  without adding title identity to the shared schema.
- Cheapest falsifier: one unlisted operational AST change must stop composition.
- Time box: one CI repair, one Claude audit, one composition.
- Stop condition: any surviving provenance mutation or non-reproducible historical object.

### Gates 3–5: first completion-aware party fit

- Capability: choose trainee and venue from completion, evolution, survival and resource state.
- Learned authority: descriptive trainee/venue preference only; no live authority yet.
- Transfer test: frozen Red initialization versus a zero-initialized control on separately declared
  Crystal development contexts before any sealed transfer result.
- Cheapest falsifier: a three-candidate unseen Red decision where the prior/update disagree and the
  measured outcome favors the baseline.
- Time box: one 8+6 catalog, one execution, one fit; no repeated tuning on six development rows.
- Stop condition: catalog lacks genuine choices/diversity, evidence fails to join, or development
  is choice-equivalent/underpowered.

### Gates 6–8: authority and transfer

- Capability: efficient party development inside a hierarchical player and across titles.
- Learned authority: trainee, venue, fight/rotate/heal/stop and evolution scheduling after the
  Milestone 4 gate; later goal selection and skill invocation.
- Transfer test: frozen paired Crystal protocols with identical menus, normalizers and controls.
- Cheapest falsifier: one randomized short Crystal scenario exposing missing shared information.
- Time box: one bounded skill gate or transfer protocol per checkpoint; no full replay as a probe.
- Stop condition: Red gains come from overlevelling, Crystal needs route-specific code, or two
  iterations fail the declared unseen/transfer metric.

## Universal prohibitions

Unless a newer, higher-authority protocol explicitly opens the gate, no agent may:

- execute or inspect sealed Red or Crystal cases;
- run a full game;
- query a teacher for an expected answer during outcome collection;
- replace a failed or exposed identity;
- commit ROMs, saves, datasets, models, private paths or private registry contents;
- let a verifier choose or replace the actor's action;
- describe a source-qualified prior as a trained model;
- promote authority from teacher agreement, lower training loss, one root or one successful run.
