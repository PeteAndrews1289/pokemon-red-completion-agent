## Mission check

- Capability: continue playing across story, preparation, recovery and resource
  boundaries using changed observations, not operator-selected next goals.
- Learned authority: expose real executable alternatives to the existing learner;
  record forced safety actions separately and never count them as learned choices.
- Transfer test: varied ROM-free states first, then prospectively assigned native
  sources and withheld combinations under the existing player-learning pilot.
- Cheapest falsifier: a retained loss followed by a fresh menu must permit recovery
  and re-planning without resetting the save, concealing costs or replacing the
  selected goal. Partial actions and unverifiable outcomes must still stop.
- Time box: reassess each 90-minute implementation slice; qualify the smallest
  native scenario before collecting a batch. Do not expand a failed pilot.
- Stop condition: major architecture/safety issue or unresolved source eligibility
  that prevents the required comparison. Preserve all work and report the exact
  missing prerequisite; never relax partitions or reopen consumed trials.

## Execution and acceptance

1. Reuse the current option loop, readiness projection, battler, goal bindings and
   recorder. Enumerate fresh observations after every settled action, including
   a declared recoverable setback. Unknown errors are not normal game losses.
2. Declare total action/frame/time/cash limits before input. Persist menu, choice,
   model identity, authority, before/after state, verification and actual costs.
   Safety actions and singleton actions are support, not comparative learning.
3. Connect coherent live readiness to actual inference and native recording.
   Test changed-state re-planning, legitimate earning/spending, recovery, budget
   exhaustion and failed/partial records. Qualify on an eligible isolated source.
4. Authenticate sources before claiming them; descendants inherit ancestry and
   partition. Known bank metadata alone does not establish independent evaluation.
   Source investigation is metadata-only until exact access is authorized.
5. Follow [the existing pilot](player-learning-pilot.md): maximum 24 TRAIN lessons,
   no more than six per decision family; stop after eight attempts if fewer than
   four are admissible. Freeze each case and its execution caps before dispatch.
6. Fit one candidate with old-corpus retention. Freeze one comparison of at most
   eight paired cases from at least two genuinely disjoint eligible source groups,
   unused in candidate or baseline fitting. Retain rejection/ties as well as wins.

Step 5 authority qualification and step 6 acquisition expansion remain separate.
The engineering completion and learning results must be reported independently.
