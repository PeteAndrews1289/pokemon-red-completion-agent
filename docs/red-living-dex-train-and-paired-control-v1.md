# Red living-Pokédex train update and paired control V1

## Decision

The current eighteen-example option-value model is useful integration machinery, but it is not
ready for authority. Its five authentic shadow decisions produced four successful skills and one
failure while exposing severe confidence errors in both directions. The next gate is one targeted,
train-only update followed by a paired comparison against the already implemented
`CompletionFirstGoalTeacher`. None of the five development outcomes may enter fitting.

This is the shortest credible route from the current model to a decision about bounded Red
authority. It replaces repeated one-arm development cases with a prospective train/evaluate split.

## What the five cases established

| Option | Outcomes | Mean predicted success | Observed success | Interpretation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Acquire species | 2 | 99.78% | 50% | One success and one highly confident failure; under-resolved risk. |
| Develop team | 1 | 0.10% | 100% | One accepted action; extreme false-negative confidence. |
| Resupply | 2 | 99.73% | 100% | Locally aligned, but two cases cannot establish calibration. |
| **All** | **5** | **79.82%** | **80%** | Mean agreement hides the opposing errors; accuracy is 60%. |

Overall Brier score is **0.397811** and clipped log loss is **2.458979**. A constant probability
equal to the observed 80% success rate scores 0.16 and 0.500402 on these same cases, respectively,
but that constant was chosen after outcomes and is only a scale check. It is not a valid control.

The reproducible aggregate is
[the five-case audit](evidence/red-repeatable-living-dex-five-case-calibration-audit-v1-2026-09-05.json).

## Train-only correction

Freeze ten new, upstream-lineage-disjoint Red train roots before opening any outcome:

- four acquisition-focused roots;
- four party-development-focused roots;
- one storage-management root;
- one resupply root.

The fixed denominator tolerates at most two setup censors. Every settled selected-arm outcome is
included; rows are never selected by result. A fit may occur only if at least eight rows settle,
including at least three acquisition and three development rows. The fit consumes the complete
eighteen-row prefix plus every settled new train row, preserves all censored rows in its dataset
identity, and remains shadow-only.

This allocation tests the two observed error modes while retaining safety-resource coverage. It
does not assume which trials will succeed, does not balance outcomes after observation, and does
not use development evidence as labels or hyperparameter guidance.

Before fitting, report:

- settled and censored counts by option kind;
- verified successes and failures without private lineage identity;
- feature-rank and condition diagnostics;
- whether acquisition and development each contain enough settled variation to identify a useful
  update;
- exact corpus and prior-model digests privately, with path-free public receipts.

If the denominator misses its minimums, stop. Do not replace a root after its outcome is known and
do not fit a smaller convenient subset.

## Fresh paired Red control

Before any evaluation outcome, freeze at least eight Red development roots that are disjoint from
all train roots and from the five opened development cases. Each root yields two independently
reset copies of the same authenticated initial state:

1. the updated title-neutral option-value model;
2. `CompletionFirstGoalTeacher` with its current frozen thresholds and weights.

Both first decisions are durably committed before either arm executes. The model and control see
the same semantic question but never private bindings, route identity, candidate position, title
identity, or the other arm's result. Timing and RNG siblings remain one evaluation lineage.

The primary paired endpoint is realized living-Pokédex utility under the existing fixed utility
contract. Report verified success, completion gain, dependency gain, irreversible loss, resource,
party and storage cost, actions, frames, typed failure, and subsequent semantic replan as separate
components. A model win must not hide a new irreversible loss or safety failure behind aggregate
utility.

Eight roots are a developmental falsifier, not broad proof. The candidate can justify only narrow
bounded-development authority if it produces a statistically valid paired advantage under the
predeclared comparison, has no model-only irreversible loss, and does not regress acquisition or
party-development behavior. Otherwise reject or redesign it; do not tune on the eight evaluation
outcomes and call the retry fresh.

## Implementation order

1. Build one action-free inventory that proves ten eligible train roots plus eight separate
   development roots exist with the declared kind coverage.
2. Freeze the complete train and paired-development schedule once, including censor rules and the
   exact deterministic control definition.
3. Collect the ten train assignments once; retain every terminal.
4. Run the existing complete-denominator fitter once if the frozen admission gate passes.
5. Commit both policy decisions for every development pair before opening outcomes.
6. Execute the eight pairs, score once, and make one promote/reject decision.
7. Only a pass may open a separately scoped multi-decision Red episode. Crystal remains later and
   uses the same semantic feature/action boundary.

## Stop conditions

Stop this gate immediately if it requires any of the following:

- fitting one of the five development outcomes;
- reusing an upstream lineage across train and development;
- treating a timing/RNG copy as an independent root;
- changing the deterministic control after an outcome;
- selecting rows, kinds or roots based on success;
- a route-, map- or species-specific policy feature;
- teacher action selection or fallback;
- sealed Red, Crystal, or a full-game replay;
- authority from training loss or unpaired success count alone.

## Mission link

The reusable capability is semantic choice among acquisition, development, storage, supply and
other living-Pokédex goals. Deterministic code retains navigation, battle mechanics, capture input,
inventory mechanics, verification and safety. The model earns only high-level choice authority,
and only through fresh paired outcomes. The same title-neutral boundary is the unit that later
transfers to Crystal; no Red path or species identity becomes a learned feature.
