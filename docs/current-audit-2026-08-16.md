## Why the lane changed

The 14-question / 55-trial one-shot campaign protected evidence but produced no learning result.
Its first authorized start ended as an immutable pre-input failure. The exact records remain valid,
consumed identities remain non-retryable, and nothing private is deleted. The campaign is retired
only as the development leading edge because its provenance and authorization cost overwhelms its
feedback rate.

Routine development is now repeatable and uses authenticated non-sealed scenarios. Benchmark rigor
starts only after a useful signal exists. Sealed work retains exact source/CI binding, external
review, per-case owner authorization, and no retry.

## Enforced anti-drift behavior

The checker fails when:

- the contract has other than one active lane;
- learning lacks outcome, model-fit, or unseen-evaluation outputs;
- maintenance does not name the learning lane it unblocks;
- the time box exceeds three sessions or stop conditions disappear;
- the development lane drops a protected-action prohibition;
- the 60/25/15 allocation shifts too far from data and modeling;
- a full replay, repeated CI-only repair, fixed-route patch, or copied teacher label is permitted;
- a nonzero progress counter lacks a tracked path-free evidence file and SHA-256;
- the generated page or required discovery links drift; or
- the pull-request mission check loses capability, authority, transfer, falsifier, time-box, or
  stop-condition fields.

## Operating policy

- 60% of a session: data and scenarios.
- 25%: model fitting and evaluation.
- 15%: maintenance and documentation.
- One session without measured learning output triggers reassessment.
- One consecutive CI-only repair triggers reassessment.
- Development does not wait for routine external review.
- Claude audits benchmark/sealed statistics and evidence.
- Antigravity challenges architecture and transfer at milestone decisions with at most three
  falsifiable claims and explicit work to delete.
