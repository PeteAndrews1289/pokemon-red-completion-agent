## What makes one root independent

Every world must:

- begin from clean emulator power in a distinct process episode;
- make its immutable train, development, or contingency assignment before controller input;
- use a distinct prospectively frozen seed and pre-controller wait;
- load no parent save state;
- write exactly one terminal state on success and at most one on any claimed attempt;
- retain the trajectory-prefix, state, envelope, physical-root, and consumption identities; and
- reach its assigned authentic three-option menu in its assigned partition.

A copied state, RNG nudge, renamed file, or rehashed envelope does not create a lineage. Admission
also rejects repeated assignment claims, upstream lineage identities, physical roots,
trajectories, states, or envelopes.
