# September27: Brock, with the loss left in the story

The earlier comparison had tied. Instead of training again to manufacture a
winner, we kept the baseline and asked whether it could continue playing.

This time the small pieces had to survive their consequences together. Training
used health and PP. A Forest trainer blocked the route. Poison caused a field
blackout while returning for healing. Reaching Pewter did not automatically start
the gym: the model had to select that challenge as a separate opportunity.

Its first Brock challenge lost. The native blackout took money but preserved
experience. The model selected a rematch from the resulting state and won. Badge,
TM34, native event and payout were verified before the party returned to a Center.
The story then continued through a supported Weedle capture, an interrupted
restoration and another regional decision, ending healthy on Route3.

There were repairs along the way, and that matters to the claim. Eight bounded
packets preserved one sequence of game states; this was not an error-free run
and not eight independent demonstrations. Eleven genuine goal selections and55
learned combat actions were separated from six sole-option support selections.
Navigation, capture mechanics, native blackout settlement and forced healing
were still provided by the system. No model weights changed.

The useful result was not a perfect Brock win rate. It was the ability to keep
the loss, pay its cost, observe the changed party and resources, and make another
consequential decision without replacing the save with a better one. The missing
cave and later-story connections remain work; reaching Route3 does not mean
Cerulean is complete, much less that the final Red run has passed.
