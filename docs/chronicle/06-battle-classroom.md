## The teacher was allowed to construct the lesson

Pete clarified that no cheating in the final player did not prohibit disclosed
training interventions. An isolated teacher could adjust money or construct
battle conditions to exercise a mechanic. The player itself must never receive
a save-editing action, and assisted resources must not be labeled earned income.

The [September 17 battle factory](../evidence/red-teacher-battle-practice-factory-2026-09-17.json)
made this concrete: declared HP, PP, moves and native outcomes could be read back
and measured. One configured example was not automatically fit-eligible or a
natural generalization result.

The classroom expanded toward species, levels, stats, moves and teams. This made
varied practice possible without waiting for the route to produce every useful
encounter. It also made source grouping essential: many edited children of one
state are not many independent origins.

## The continuation behind a label changed what “best” meant

Earlier labels could reward an opening action followed by a strong teacher.
The deployed learner had to make the subsequent choices itself. If teacher and
learner continuations differ, the opening that looks best under one may not be
best under the other.

The later J work retained these distinctions. Its generated-team comparison
improved from 7/24 to 9/24 wins but still lost most cases and used more decisions.
Natural early-game tests supplied a different kind of evidence. Two origins
reached Brock; later switching probes required the reserve to be used rather
than accepting a win that left it untouched.

The [September 19 integration report](../work-sessions/2026-09-19-player-battler-integration.md)
records a more sobering first player connection. Four learned decisions were
followed by a Wartortle faint. A strict no-faints funding guard stopped the battle
before replacement. The payout remained zero.

The [retained continuation](../evidence/red-battle-lifecycle-continuation-2026-09-19.json)
then lost and blacked out. The cash loss and terminal state were preserved.
The lesson was a contract distinction: story combat must be allowed to finish
through ordinary fainting, while stricter funding qualification can remain failed.

## Larger parties and the limits of the successful comparison

K's continuation-aware control update kept existing retention checks and improved
several TRAIN measurements. In eight paired battles both J and K won all cases;
K used fewer decisions and PP and had fewer faints, while total HP loss increased.
That is a mixed measured result, not a perfect-policy claim.

[Two natural six-member origins](../evidence/red-natural-six-qualification-2026-09-20.json)
then verified actual late-slot use. Across the measured cases both models won;
K used fewer decisions but suffered 16 faints against J's 15. The enemy roster
was the same early workload. Six-member execution was demonstrated; broad mastery
was not.

## Earn, heal, spend—and keep the failed searches

The [earned-spending integration](../evidence/red-k-earned-spending-integration-2026-09-20.json)

a paid Safari entry. A retained continuation caught Rhyhorn without charging
another admission fee.

This was the desired relationship between battling and collection: money earned
through play made another goal possible. The result did not erase earlier route,
evolution or search failures.

The [League recovery record](../evidence/red-league-recovery-2026-09-20.json)
showed recovery to a finish, but another Champion loss and blackout left a
3,504 net cost. Renewable League profitability remained unqualified. “Can beat
the League” and “can reliably fund collection there” were still different claims.
