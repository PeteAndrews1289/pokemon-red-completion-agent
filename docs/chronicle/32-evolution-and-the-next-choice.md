# September27: evolution is progress,not yet learning

The same player that had beaten Brock continued through Mt. Moon. It chose some
training,fought with the learned battler,lost three trainer battles and kept
the consequences. Later,poison caused another blackout while it was trying to
reach healing. No better save replaced those states.

Two ordinary game events exposed an extraordinary amount of bookkeeping:
Squirtle became Wartortle,and Weedle became Kakuna. The system first canceled
evolution while settling a won battle. After that was corrected,a preservation
check rejected the changed species as missing Pokémon. The repair had to prove
these were the same earned individuals,not simply remove the protection.

That observation failure also interrupted a goal receipt. The actual terminal,
action traces,battle results and money loss were retained and independently
reconciled without replay. The original goal never received a retroactive
success label. This was engineering recovery,not a magically clean run.

The party eventually reached Cerulean fully restored,carrying Helix Fossil.
Across this section there were229learned combat choices and ten genuine goal
choices. Most route steps had only one executable option and were support.
The fossil identity itself was chosen by a least-cost mechanic,not by the model.

The more consequential finding was in the policy:all252new readiness weights
were zero. The player could select training,but that did not establish learned
anticipation of a stronger opponent. Its party gained levels;its weights did
not change. Misty's forecast had come from a disclosed cartridge preview.

At Cerulean,Pete chose the next question deliberately:let the model choose
between distinct story branches instead of having our code choose Misty or Bill
first. Existing plural-menu components offer a starting point. But two options
with different names are not enough if their consequences look identical to
the learner. Proving that distinction is the next milestone.
