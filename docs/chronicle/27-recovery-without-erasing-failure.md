# September 26: recovery without erasing the failure

The earlier comparison exposed things a capable battler cannot fix: runs sharing
action-record identities and a preparation skill arriving too injured to train.
The next integration made executions distinct, connected real arrival health to
legitimate healing and preserved the original training target. It also separated
finishing the local curriculum from having no actions because something broke.

The most useful evidence came from failure. An excursion returned to the Center
at4/22HP without reaching level7. A declared continuation used the remaining
original budget, healed to22/22 and reached level7. No new target or rewritten save.
Healing was disclosed deterministic support inside the previously selected goal,
not newly learned authority.

Even that result lacked a clean ending: the private helper failed serializing its
report. States, sealed actions and battle receipts survived. Independent readback
verified recovery and target attainment while preserving the report failure and
earlier failed training dose. No replay replaced the inconvenient result.

Eleven initial tests produced five ordinary local completions and one completion
on a disclosed teacher-speed fixture. Others retained partial failures, an
unavailable menu, an XP-measurement error and a deliberate budget stop. All share
one TRAIN origin and cannot establish a general success rate. Initial travel
injury remains regression-tested; native recovery used actual arrival after training.

No fit or promotion occurred. Autonomous recovery after every partial failure,
resource coverage and a fresh independent comparison remain. The player is not
finished; its connected parts can now pay a real recovery cost and resume an
unchanged intent without erasing the setback.
