# September23 — a real loss, then a learned choice to try again

The first learned Lorelei attempt came extremely close. Four opponents fell;
Lapras survived with2of220HP. The party blacked out, lost13673cash, retained7842XP
and recovered normally. The logged48choices were real model actions, not teacher
substitutions. That result did not establish that the party simply needed levels.

Development briefly returned to machinery: battle-aftermath features, a bounded
preparation offer and reentry behavior. A wrong projection was caught on an
isolated copy; the existing lobby-reset logic provided the correct shared fix.
The failed tests remain evidence, not erased attempts or independent roots.

During the larger audit, we distinguished an available input interface from a
trained policy. The new loss-aware policy had not been trained. Rather than
block another supported learned choice on that research project, we explicitly
used the frozen existing goal model's current-state features. It saw both an
executable training outing and a story attempt, and chose the story attempt.

This time the learned battler defeated all five opponents in53decisions, using
all five party positions and one status action. No teacher took over. The earned
campaign continued from the actual recovered loss—not an earlier save or a
favorable diagnostic copy. The reward was5544cash and10471XP; story progress
became31of36. All goal/battle choices were independently reconstructed without
emulator input.

The victory was expensive: four fainted teammates, Blastoise38HP and no Surf PP.
We stopped in Lorelei's room rather than treat winning one battle as evidence of
League readiness. The next question is legitimate between-room recovery and
supplies, not a promised effortless run through the remaining opponents.

## What this does and does not establish

The model chose and executed a useful next goal after a real setback. It did not
newly learn a causal grinding rule; its old input lacks the detailed aftermath.
No new weights or registrations were produced, and the unplayed preparation arm
has no outcome label. One win after one loss cannot identify chance, accumulated
XP or policy choices as the sole cause. The complete Red gate remains unpassed.
