# September27: the better fit did not become the better player

The previous chapter ended with a promising training result:the learner had
finally begun to distinguish immediate challenges. The next question was whether
that distinction helped it play.

First,the connected menu ran on a retained mechanics copy. The model chose
grinding,story movement,healing and a rival attempt. It earned experience and
reached its existing level target,but lost the rival battle. Nothing replaced
the real save,and the loss remained part of the record.

Then two separately initialized test origins supplied four paired conditions.
The baseline and the164-example candidate played eight bounded attempts,using
the same battle model and executable choices. All attempts settled without
execution errors. Neither model completed Misty or Bill within the limits.

The candidate made36goal choices to the baseline's24 and won more wild battles.
It also caught one Rattata. But it earned only one new trainer milestone against
four,blacked out seven times against four,and lost936cash against749. One
baseline attempt beat the rival after two retained losses and earned the normal
595payout. The candidate failed the predeclared adoption rule.

The comparison was deliberately limited:assisted single-Wartortle fixtures,
two origin clusters,not the actual four-member Cerulean party. Until native
Center healing,their blackout destination remained Pallet. These limitations
stay with the result;they were not corrected after seeing which model won.

The audit also retained a reporting defect:one final battle was missing from a
summary list,but its complete native log and matching final-goal outcome existed.
Those records independently reconstructed the missing entry without replay.

The lesson is not that learning is pointless. It is that lower training error
cannot substitute for useful decisions. The model sees the next executable
action;an easy exit from a building is not a valuation of the quest beyond it.
A zero-input probe confirmed that distinction,without proving it explains every
loss. The next direction is short-chain planning and useful preparation,not
another blind fit,more level inflation or a fixed order disguised as autonomy.

The accepted baseline,original healthy Cerulean save and older history remain
intact. The ten-step goal is unfinished. This is an honest failed promotion test,
not a fresh Red completion and not lost prior work.
