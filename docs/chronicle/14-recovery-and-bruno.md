# September23 — recovery buys another battle, not a restart

Lorelei left only Blastoise conscious, with38HP and no Surf PP. Rather than
reset the League, Pete approved testing the actual remaining items on a copy.
The owned Rare Candy legitimately revived Farfetch'd through level-up HP gain.
Four Potions, four Super Potions and one Elixir then restored two usable members.
The learned battler defeated Bruno, leaving both alive.

The first test also caught a verification mistake: full collection equality
incorrectly rejected the intended level increase. We retained that failed check
and its post-item save, verified the exact change, and continued from that state.
No item was restored or battle replayed to hide the failure.

After the successful copy, Pete authorized actual campaign integration. The
corrected verifier allows only the intended specimen level change; the earned
attempt executed native menus and combat from the real post-Lorelei save.
Eleven learned decisions—seven attacks and four switch prompts—defeated all five
opponents without another faint. Blastoise reached55, full172HP and5Surf PP;
Farfetch'd46 retained77HP. Three other teammates remained fainted.
The campaign earned5742cash and9032battleXP, reaching32/36story objectives.

This was integration of an existing battler, not a new fit or learned recovery
policy. The owner-directed recovery plan and opening lead were deterministic
support; the model chose attacks and switches. Actual inputs reproduced the
copy terminal from the same source and timing, so these are not two independent
wins or a reliability estimate. Three Super Potions remained; play stopped
before Agatha. One successful recovery does not establish whole-League readiness.
