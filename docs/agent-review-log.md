# Review decisions and evidence

Updated November 19, 2026.

Pete directed the project and the acceptance criteria. Codex, Claude and Antigravity contributed engineering or review at different points. A suggestion earned credit when a retained comparison or native replay supported it. This public chronology groups related decisions and records unresolved results.

The earlier published review record remains available in Git. [History through November 2](https://github.com/PeteAndrews1289/pokemon-red-completion-agent/blob/43f442b924da12aa8474e74a061ad441c6032de6/docs/agent-review-log.md)

## November 2–5 · Goal selection and repetition

A copy escaped a repeated movement sequence and reached Surf. A broad exploration policy also interrupted promising story choices and failed the original midgame and late-game tests. The project retained the unsuccessful comparison and narrowed the proposed escape to witnessed recurrence. [Evidence](work-sessions/2026-11-05-story-continuation.md)

## November 6–9 · Goal continuity, training venues and host limits

Observations of early Safari exits and low-level battles led to separate measurements of venue, travel, healing and the intended trainee’s experience. Four-player tests also exposed host memory pressure. The dashboard and monitoring work made operator interventions visible alongside game outcomes. [Evidence](work-sessions/2026-11-08-train-recipe-four-campaigns.json)

## November 10–13 · Whole objectives and preparation

Reviewers asked whether a choice survived a chunk boundary and whether the selected Pokémon actually earned the experience. Comparisons extended through a whole story objective. Venue and stopping tests distinguished short local gains from independent evidence of a better model. [Evidence](work-sessions/2026-11-13-venue-multitiming-train.json)

## November 14–16 · Funding, battle setbacks and late-game resources

Testing followed the entire sequence from earning money to buying supplies and registering a species. Combat and preparation comparisons exposed differences between the collector and the player. The project retained failures and setbacks alongside successful replays and qualified each change on its own build. [Evidence](work-sessions/production-train-2026-11-16.md)

## November 17–18 · Recovery and campaign timing

The project added milestone splits and tested fresh-start binding. Late-game comparisons measured both acquisition orders through their shared endpoint. Four same-seed emulators then supplied complete trajectories with the costs of every external continuation. [Evidence](work-sessions/late-collection-comparison-2026-11-18.md)

## November 19 · A repeated failure before Brock

All four development saves reached eight badges, the Hall of Fame and 124 registrations. Their first stops shared one cartridge state. A fifth start reached it again. Two native copy tests verified the repaired poison-blackout handoff. Its live save paid ₽633 and returned the next goal choice to the model. [Evidence](work-sessions/solo-field-blackout-2026-11-19.md)
